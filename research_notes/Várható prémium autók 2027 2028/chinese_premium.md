# Chinese premium / upper-mid EVs and PHEVs (D/E segment) for Europe and Hungary, 2025 to 2028

Research date: 2026-10-31. Buyer context: a company near Győr replacing a 2020 Audi A6 50 TDI and a VW Arteon Shooting Brake in 1 to 2 years (about late 2027 to late 2028). They want real 130 km/h highway range and fast charging.

Confidence labels used below:
- **OFFICIAL**: the maker's own site or press release, the European Commission, or an EU decision.
- **PRESS**: credible trade press (electrive, CnEVPost, CarNewsChina, InsideEVs, Autocar and similar outlets).
- **RUMOR**: anonymous sources, analysts' estimates, or secondary aggregators.
- **FORUM**: social media or forum posts.

HUF figures are rough conversions at about 355 HUF/EUR, the rate implied by totalcar.hu in May 2026, unless a Hungarian list price is quoted.

## Q1. Which Chinese premium D/E-segment models are already on sale in Europe (launched 2025 to 2026), and what are their specs and prices?

### Takeaway
The strongest Audi A6 / Arteon SB replacements already on sale are three cars. The **Zeekr 7GT** shooting brake (800V, 480 kW, up to 655 km WLTP, from about €46–48k) holds the record in Bjørn Nyland's 1,000 km test. The **Xpeng P7+** liftback (from €46.6k, 446 kW, built in Austria) is the second. The **MG/IM IM5** saloon and **IM6** SUV (800V, up to 710 km WLTP, from €53,990 in Germany) are the third. BYD's European D-segment offer is the Sealion 7 BEV (230 kW at best) and the Seal 6 DM-i Touring PHEV estate. Neither matches the 800V cars on charging speed.

### Inferences
- For an Arteon SB driver, the closest like-for-like body style is the **Zeekr 7GT** (shooting brake, available now), followed by the **NIO ET5 Touring**. **Denza Z9 GT** is a far more expensive E-segment shooting brake at about €100–115k. The Seal 6 Touring is a PHEV with weak 26 kW DC charging, so it does not meet the "fast charging" brief.
- For an A6 driver wanting a saloon or liftback: Xpeng P7+, MG IM5 and Zeekr 001 (larger).
- Charging hardware is a clear Chinese advantage. 7GT (480 kW), P7+ (446 kW), IM5/IM6 (800V) and Zeekr 7X (800V) all have 10–80% times of 12–16 min. That is well ahead of the BYD Sealion 7 (150–230 kW).

## Q3. What is available in Hungary specifically (importers, dealers, prices), and what will the BYD Szeged plant build?

### Takeaway

## Q4. How do the EU countervailing duties on China-made EVs affect these cars?

### Takeaway
Since 30 Oct 2024, China-made **BEVs** pay a definitive EU countervailing duty of 7.8–35.3% on top of the normal 10% import duty. The company rates are BYD 17.0%, Geely (Zeekr, Lynk, Volvo) 18.8% and SAIC (MG/IM) 35.3%. **PHEVs are not covered.** That is why Zeekr 9X, Lynk 08, Denza D9, Seal 6 Touring DM-i and Atto 2 DM-i are attractive to import. A January 2026 EU–China framework allows minimum-price "price undertakings" instead of duties. By October 2026, only one acceptance was found: VW Anhui's Cupra Tavascan, in February 2026. **EU-built Chinese cars avoid the duty**: Xpeng models from Magna Graz, Leapmotor from Zaragoza, BYD from Szeged.

### Cited Findings
- Definitive duties apply from 30 Oct 2024 for 5 years, at 7.8% to 35.3% — [ibonomics summary](https://www.ibonomics.org/real-world-examples-paper-1-ib-economics/eu-countervailing-duties-chinese-evs); [iEVChina](https://ievchina.com/insights/eu-ev-tariff-on-chinese-cars-2026-latest-rates-buyer-price-impact/).
- Original rates: BYD 17%, Geely 18.8%, SAIC 35.3% — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3339914/china-eu-tariff-agreement-evs-seen-cutting-shipments-boosting-profitability) (via search summary).
- EU guidance on price-undertaking offers was published on 12 Jan 2026. Minimum prices are derived from historical CIF prices adjusted for the duty margin, or from comparable EU-made BEVs (OFFICIAL via PRESS) — [electrive, 2026-01-12](https://www.electrive.com/2026/01/12/good-bye-tariffs-eu-publishes-guidance-on-minimum-price-mechanism-with-china/); [Caixin, 2026-01-13](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-01-13/china-eu-reach-breakthrough-to-end-ev-tariffs-102403228.html); [Trivium China](https://triviumchina.com/2026/01/13/eu-and-china-reach-important-milestone-on-ev-price-undertakings/).
- First accepted undertaking: VW (Anhui) for the Cupra Tavascan, decision (EU) 2026/328 of 9 Feb 2026, announced 10 Feb 2026. It can be withdrawn and the duties reinstated retroactively (OFFICIAL) — [European Commission DG Trade, 2026-02-10](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-accepts-price-undertaking-chinese-electric-car-producer-2026-02-10_en); [Borderlex](https://borderlex.net/2026/02/10/commission-agrees-first-price-undertaking-with-china-based-ev-maker/).
- China's Ministry of Commerce expects more firms to reach undertakings. No second acceptance (BYD, Geely, SAIC and others) was found in searches up to 2026-10-31.
- Analysis says price undertakings bring "scant benefits, significant risks" — [Bruegel](https://www.bruegel.org/first-glance/scant-benefits-significant-risks-price-undertakings-chinese-electric-vehicles-entering).
- PHEVs are not subject to the higher tariff. BYD keeps the Atto 2 PHEV China-built for this reason — [hvg.hu, 2026-02-09](https://hvg.hu/cegauto/20260209_negy-modell-keszulhet-byd-szeged-gyar).
- Xpeng P7+ and G9L are built at Magna Graz, Austria — [CnEVPost, 2026-01-05](https://cnevpost.com/2026-01-05/xpeng-austria-plant-completes-trial-production-2026-p7-plus/); [CarNewsChina, 2026-10-12](https://carnewschina.com/2026/10/12/xpeng-g9l-ev-crossover-launches-in-europe-at-83850-usd/).

### Inferences
- **Price impact.** Zeekr 7GT and 7X (Geely, China-built) carry about 18.8% plus 10%. MG IM5/IM6 carry about 35.3% plus 10%, yet are still priced at €54–61k in Germany. This suggests these makers absorb part of the duty. If price undertakings are later accepted for Geely or SAIC, list prices may **not fall**, because a minimum price is the mechanism. The difference is that the margin goes to the maker instead of EU customs.
- Hungary has no extra national import levy on these cars beyond EU rules, as far as found. The duty is already in the list price.
- That the Zeekr 7GT is built in China (Ningbo) is an inference from the PMA2+ / 007GT sharing. No source in this session states the plant.

### Gaps
- No status update (September or October 2026) on undertaking applications by BYD, Geely or SAIC.
- The 20.7% "other cooperating" rate and the 7.8% Tesla rate come from the 2024 regulation and were not re-verified this session.

## Q5. Real-world highway range (130 km/h) and fast-charging results

### Takeaway
The **Zeekr 7GT** holds the best independent result. In Bjørn Nyland's 1,000 km challenge, the 7GT Privilege AWD set a record of **8 h 50 min** for a battery car (excluding NIO swap cars). It reached a 400 kW charging peak and its net battery measured 100.6 kWh. The 7GT Core RWD did 8 h 55 min at 216 Wh/km. German tests put realistic 120–130 km/h range at about **400 km**, with 21–23 kWh/100 km. No comparable independent highway data was found for the Xpeng P7+, IM5/IM6, Sealion 7 or Denza in this session.

### Cited Findings
- Zeekr 7GT Privilege AWD (475 kW, 100 kWh) completed Bjørn Nyland's 1,000 km challenge in **8:50 h**. It hit 400 kW at the first stop. Nyland measured 100.6 kWh net. It was faster than all tested BEVs except NIO battery-swap models. The BMW iX3 50 xDrive was slower. Dated 10 July, year not shown, presumably 2026 (PRESS) — [InsideEVs.de](https://insideevs.de/news/801274/zeekr-7gt-rekord-1000km-challenge/); [elektroquatsch](https://www.elektroquatsch.de/artikel/zeekr-7gt-1000-km-challenge-rekord-800-volt-420-kw-laden).
- Zeekr 7GT Core RWD (75 kWh LFP): 1,000 km in **8:55 h** at 216 Wh/km. Mercedes CLA 350 4Matic: 9:00 h at 202 Wh/km (FORUM / X post summarising Nyland) — [X post](https://x.com/TSLA_inside_/status/2085604039018922307).
- ElektroQuatsch 1,000 km test (7GT Core): 21.6 kWh/100 km average, first charge stop after 408 km. "Over 400 km realistic at 120 km/h" (PRESS) — [elektroquatsch](https://www.elektroquatsch.de/artikel/zeekr-7gt-core-top-10-1000-km-test-laden-verbrauch).
- Carwow DE (7GT AWD): about 400 km at 120–130 km/h in mild weather, about 23 kWh/100 km in testing (PRESS) — [carwow.de](https://www.carwow.de/zeekr/7-gt).
- Caradisiac: 19 kWh/100 km on a mixed route mostly at 130 km/h — [caradisiac](https://www.caradisiac.com/au-volant-de-la-zeekr-7gt-confortable-mais-depassee-par-ses-646-ch-222616.htm).
- EV-Database estimates for the 7GT Long Range RWD at 110 km/h: about 525 km in summer, 405 km in winter (modelled) — [ev-database](https://ev-database.org/de/pkw/3429/Zeekr-7GT-Long-Range-RWD).
- BMW iX3 (Neue Klasse) for reference: 8:55 h, best SUV result, at freezing temperatures — [bmwblog](https://www.bmwblog.com/2026/04/28/bmw-ix3-na5-1000km-test-result/).

### Inferences
- Rule of thumb from the 7GT data: at 130 km/h, real-world range is about 60–65% of WLTP. Applied to the other cars (inference only, not measured):
  - IM5 RWD 710 km WLTP → about 430–460 km.
  - P7+ LR 530 km → about 320–350 km.
  - Sealion 7 Excellence 502 km → about 300–330 km.
  - Zeekr 7X LR 627 km → about 380–400 km.
- For repeated Győr–Budapest–Vienna–Munich business trips, charging speed matters as much as range. The 400–480 kW cars (7GT, P7+, 7X, IM) will usually need one 12–16 min stop per 300–350 km leg.
- Denza Flash Charging (1 MW+) depends on BYD building megawatt chargers in Europe and Hungary. 3,000 are planned in Europe. Real-world availability in Hungary by 2028 is unknown.

### Gaps
- No Bjørn Nyland or ADAC highway results found this session for the Xpeng P7+, IM5/IM6, Sealion 7, NIO ET5 Touring, Zeekr 7X/001 or Denza Z9 GT. ADAC Ecotest results were not retrieved.

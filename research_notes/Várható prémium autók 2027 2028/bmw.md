# BMW premium D/E segment: models launching or starting European deliveries, Nov 2026 – end 2028

Research date: 2026-10-31. Customer context: Hungarian company near Győr, current cars 2020 Audi A6 50 TDI and VW Arteon Shooting Brake 2.0 TDI 4Motion, switch in 1–2 years (roughly late 2027 to late 2028), main interest EVs with real highway range, PHEV/ICE also considered.

Confidence labels used: **OFFICIAL** (BMW press release, configurator or BMW site) / **CREDIBLE PRESS** (named outlet reporting announced facts or reliable insider data) / **SPY/INSIDER RUMOR** (spy shots, unnamed insiders) / **FORUM** (Bimmerpost or other forum posts).

Note on sources: many results came through search-engine summaries. The pages fetched in full were the BMW i3 production press release (newsroom.bmwgroup.com, 6 Aug 2026), the BMW UK iX4 press release (8 Oct 2026), the BMWBlog X5 configurator article (10 Oct 2026), the BMWBlog X5 powertrain article (15 Jun 2026), and the Wikipedia pages for the i3 (NA0) and iX4. Wikipedia's figures are internally inconsistent in places and are used only where press sources back them.

## Q1: Neue Klasse rollout and status of each D/E-segment model (launched vs upcoming)

### Takeaway
By 31 Oct 2026 three Neue Klasse models have been revealed: the iX3 (NA5, Debrecen, on sale since late 2025), the i3 sedan (NA0, Munich, built since Aug 2026, European deliveries autumn 2026) and the iX4 (NA7, Debrecen, revealed 8 Oct 2026, deliveries from March 2027). Those in the Nov 2026–2028 window are: iX3 M60 and iX4 M60 (spring 2027), the new G65 X5/iX5 (Spartanburg; ICE on sale late Nov 2026, iX5/PHEV early or spring 2027), the i3 Touring (NA1, Munich, production 2H 2027 at the earliest), the G50 combustion 3 Series (Dingolfing, production Nov 2026, dealers early 2027, PHEV within a year), the G51 3 Series Touring (ICE/PHEV, 2027), the G60 5 Series/i5 LCI (production July 2027, still on CLAR, not Gen6), the iX5 Hydrogen (2028), and the next X6 (G66, about April 2028). The 7 Series LCI (G70) is already revealed and in production (April 2026). The i4 is ending, and BMW treats the i3 as its successor.

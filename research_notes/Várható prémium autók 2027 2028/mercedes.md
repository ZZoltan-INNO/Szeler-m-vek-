## Electric C-Class timeline and variants

### Takeaway
The electric C-Class (C 400 4MATIC, 94 kWh, up to 762 km WLTP, 330 kW DC, 800V) is already launched. It is built in Kecskemét: production started 13 July 2026, deliveries started around September 2026, and orders placed now may wait until Q1 2027. A rear-wheel-drive version is officially confirmed but has no name or date yet. Smaller-battery versions and an AMG model are expected later.

### Cited Findings
- German price: C 400 4MATIC €67,711 incl. VAT (€56,900 net). Air suspension with rear-axle steering is €2,944. Comparison prices from the same article: GLC 400 4MATIC €71,281, C 300 e €68,907, C 300 de €71,287, C 400 e €73,429. Electrive also says the car can pass €100,000 with options. (electrive, 2026-05-06) — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/05/06/electric-mercedes-c-class-starts-at-e67711/)
- Specs: 94 kWh battery, 360 kW, up to 762 km WLTP, 330 kW DC, MB.EA-M 800V architecture, two-speed rear e-motor (shared with the CLA), 4.90 m long. AC charging is 11 kW as standard, with a 22 kW option at €654.50. Charging at 400V stations needs an optional 800V-to-400V DC-DC converter (€654.50). (electrive, 2026-05-06) — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/05/06/electric-mercedes-c-class-starts-at-e67711/)
- Future variants (electrive, 2026-05-06) — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/05/06/electric-mercedes-c-class-starts-at-e67711/):
  - RWD model: officially confirmed, no name yet. Electrive expects the 94 kWh battery, a 200 kW motor and about 800 km range, possibly named "C 300+". These details are electrive's speculation.
  - "Various battery variants" are planned later, presumably including a cheaper smaller battery.
  - AMG version: rumored, not confirmed by Mercedes.
- Production in Kecskemét started 13 July 2026. Deliveries were expected from September, with possible waits into Q1 2027 — [electrive search summary / LatestEVNews](https://www.latestevnews.com/articles/mercedes-c-class-eq-production-starts-in-hungary-deliveries-from-september-wait-times-could-extend-into-2027); [TechTimes, 2026-07-16](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320743/20260716/mercedes-begins-building-electric-c-class-762-km-range-two-speed-rear-axle.htm)

It says the car is built in Kecskemét's new southern hall for nearly all world markets, and that China gets a locally built long-wheelbase "L" version. The length differs from electrive's 4.90 m. — [Magyar Nemzet](https://magyarnemzet.hu/auto-motor-2/2026/07/ezeket-a-tipusokat-fogja-gyartani-kecskemeten-europa-legnagyobb-mercedes-gyara)

AMS added that it was unclear whether the C and E would also come as estates. — [auto motor und sport, 2025-10-07](https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/neuheiten/mercedes-glc-eq-elektro-suv-erhaelt-keine-coupe-variante/)

### Inferences
- The C 400 4MATIC's German price (€67.7k) is close to the C 300 e PHEV. Converted at roughly 390-400 HUF/EUR, the Hungarian list price would likely be around 27-29 M HUF. This is an estimate: no Hungarian C EV list price was found.
- A C-Class EV estate (T-Modell) would be the natural replacement for the Arteon Shooting Brake. No source confirms one, so treat it as unknown.
- The RWD long-range C (about 800 km by electrive's estimate) could be the best highway-range option in this lineup if it arrives in 2027.

### Gaps

- No confirmation of an electric C-Class Estate/T-Modell.
- The W520 code comes only from Magyar Nemzet and is not verified against a Mercedes source.

## GLC with EQ Technology: additional variants (RWD GLC 300+, Coupé, AMG)

### Takeaway
The electric GLC is launched. As of June 2026 the European lineup is GLC 250 (RWD, 85 kWh, 647 km), GLC 300 4MATIC (85 kWh, 613 km) and GLC 400 4MATIC (94 kWh, 673 km, or 715 km with the "Long Range Edition"). The 250 and 300 4MATIC deliver from about end-2026. A GLC 300+ (RWD, 94 kWh) and an entry RWD with a 64 kWh battery are reported for around year-end. Mercedes said in Oct 2025 that no electric GLC Coupé is planned, so the GLC Coupé stays PHEV/ICE (C254). An AMG GLC EV with three axial-flux motors and over 900 hp is in testing.

- June 2026 lineup and German prices (electrive, 2026-06-09) — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/06/09/mercedes-opens-order-books-for-two-additional-battery-electric-glc-variants/):

## Electric E-Class (EQE successor) and E-Class W214 facelift

### Takeaway
The electric E-Class ("E-Klasse mit EQ Technologie") replaces the EQE sedan. It was moved to MB.EA-M after the MB.EA-L platform was cancelled, and brought forward to a 2027 launch (H2 2027 market launch; IAA Sept 2027 debut rumored). It has a classic three-box shape, about 5 m long, with 800V and up to 350 kW charging; over 700 km WLTP is expected but not confirmed. The combustion/PHEV W214 continues alongside it, with a facelift expected "from 2027 at the earliest". I found no spy shots or dates for the facelift.

### Cited Findings
- Mercedes is bringing the electric E-Class forward by a year: EQE successor launch in H2 2027 instead of 2028. (electrive, 2025-08-20) — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2025/08/20/mercedes-launches-the-electric-e-class-a-year-earlier-than-planned/)
- MB.EA-Large (planned for EQE/EQS successors) was cancelled and the E-Class EV moved to MB.EA-Medium; 800V, up to 350 kW charging, possibly the C 400 dual-motor setup; traditional three-box styling. (search summaries citing electrive, Carscoops, autoevolution) — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2025/08/20/mercedes-launches-the-electric-e-class-a-year-earlier-than-planned/); [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2285580/2027-mercedes-benz-e-class-spy-shots-nurburgring/); [autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/2028-mercedes-benz-e-class-eq-spied-for-the-first-time-in-a-rush-to-replace-the-failed-eqe-259967.html)
- InsideEVs.de spied a near-uncamouflaged prototype (dated 8 Sept, presumably 2026). Its findings — [InsideEVs.de](https://insideevs.de/news/807530/mercedes-e-klasse-elektrisch-erlkoenig/):
  - Launch in 2027, earlier than first planned; platform switched from MB.EA-L to MB.EA-M.
  - About 5 m long, similar to the BMW i5's 5.06 m; classic sedan proportions.
  - The EQE would have been on the market only 12-18 months before its successor arrives.
  - A related headline mentions an IAA 2027 debut.

## CLA / CLA Shooting Brake variants (incl. AMG CLA 45 EV) and Kecskemét's future models

### Takeaway
The CLA family (C174 sedan and X174 Shooting Brake, MMA platform, 800V EV plus a 48V hybrid) is launched. The electric Mercedes-AMG CLA 45 4MATIC+ was revealed on 9 Jul 2026 as a saloon and Shooting Brake: 500 kW, 94 kWh, over 670 km WLTP (saloon) and 640 km (Shooting Brake), with European sales "later in 2026".

Kecskemét now builds:
- the C-Class EV (from Jul 2026);
- the GLC EV (assembly starting, as the third site after Bremen and Beijing);
- the GLB;
- the outgoing A-Class, whose production ends Aug 2027.

Kecskemét is officially the sole global source of the "little G-Class" (MMA-based, 800V EV plus a later hybrid), but its timing has slipped: production now starts early 2028 and first deliveries are expected spring 2028. Sources conflict on whether the new CLA is still built in Kecskemét: Rastatt is the lead plant, and Magyar Nemzet says the CLA is not built in Hungary.

# Upcoming premium D/E-segment models, Nov 2026 – end 2028: Volvo, Polestar, Tesla, Lexus/Toyota, Hyundai/Genesis, Kia (Europe/Hungary)

Research date: 2026-10-31. Buyer context: a Hungarian company near Győr replacing a 2020 Audi A6 50 TDI and a VW Arteon Shooting Brake in 1–2 years. It wants EVs with real 130 km/h motorway range, and premium hybrids/PHEVs also count.

Confidence labels used below: **OFFICIAL** = manufacturer or official price list. **PRESS** = credible trade press. **RUMOR** = spy shots or unnamed sources. **SPEC** = forum or aggregator speculation.

Method caveat: about 25 searches and fetches. Many figures come from search-engine summaries of the linked pages, not from full-page reads. Pages read in full were Electrive (EX60), WardsAuto (Polestar), Not a Tesla App (roadmap), Electric Cars Report (Lexus ES Europe) and Elbilen (Polestar 3/4). Please re-verify prices before any purchase decision.

## Which models are actually coming to Europe/Hungary in the window (Nov 2026 – 2028), and which are already launched?

### Takeaway
Most of the "next-gen" cars on the original list are already on sale in Europe by Oct 2026: Volvo EX60, ES90, Polestar 5, updated Polestar 3/4, Lexus ES (new generation), the Hyundai Ioniq 6 facelift and the Tesla Model Y variants. Hungarian orders are open for the EX60 and the new Lexus ES.

The genuinely *upcoming* items in the window are fewer:
- Volvo EX60 Cross Country (Hungarian deliveries from mid-2027)
- Long-range XC60/XC90 PHEV updates (deliveries early 2027, confirmed for the US; Europe unconfirmed)
- Polestar 4 long-roof/"shooting brake" variant (late 2026, unconfirmed body)
- Next-gen Polestar 2 (early 2027)
- Polestar 7 (2028, compact, below the target segment)
- Genesis GV90 (Europe H1 2027, selected markets)
- Tesla Model Y L six-seater (Europe launch imminent but undated)
- A next-gen Volvo XC90 around 2028 (rumor)

## Real-world highway (130 km/h) range reputation

### Takeaway
The EX60 has the best-documented 130 km/h data. The P10 AWD uses about 22 kWh/100 km at 130 km/h, giving about 418 km. The P12 manages about 509 km. That is competitive, but slightly behind the BMW iX3 50 (about 545 km) in the same test. The ES90's motorway efficiency appears weaker, with test reports of 25–30 kWh/100 km on fast motorway stretches, and the 92 kWh car's motorway range lands in the 370–420 km area.

### Cited Findings
- **Volvo EX60 P10 AWD**, Carup (Sweden) Autobahn test:
  - 100 km/h: 17 kWh/100 km
  - **130 km/h: 22 kWh/100 km, about 418 km range**
  - 160 km/h: 33 kWh/100 km, about 280 km
  - Source: [Carup](https://carup.se/volvo-ex60-testad-pa-autobahn-verkliga-rackvidden-avslojad/)
- **EX60 P12**: about 509 km at 130 km/h, in the same YouTube roadtrip test that measured the P10 at 418 km. The same test gave the BMW iX3 50 xDrive 20 kWh/100 km and 545 km, and the Mercedes GLC EQ 400 21 kWh/100 km and 450 km. The test was run in warmer weather.
  - Source: [YouTube Autobahn roadtrip test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rSQFkL99Zgo) (figures via search summary; not watched)
- EX60 P10 test average of 18.9 kWh/100 km over a 155 km loop, about 528 km — [AUTO BILD](https://www.autobild.de/artikel/volvo-ex60-p10-awd-test-b7919cf-28806037.html)
- EX60: 566 km in a US mixed (60% city / 40% highway) range test at 17.9 kWh/100 km — [elektroquatsch.de](https://www.elektroquatsch.de/artikel/volvo-ex60-566-km-us-reichweitentest)

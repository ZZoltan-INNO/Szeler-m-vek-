# Upcoming Audi and VW Group premium D/E-segment models (Nov 2026 – end 2028)

Research date: 2026-10-31. Researcher scope: Audi, VW, Škoda, Porsche (Cupra briefly). Customer context: Hungarian company near Győr. Current cars: 2020 Audi A6 50 TDI (C8) and VW Arteon Shooting Brake 2.0 TDI 4Motion. Decision expected in 1–2 years.

Confidence labels used below:
- **OFFICIAL**: manufacturer press release or manufacturer statement
- **PRESS**: credible trade or press report, often citing insiders
- **SPY**: spy-shot based
- **SPEC**: forum or single-outlet speculation

"(bg)" marks background knowledge from before this session that I did not re-verify here. The report writer should treat (bg) items as needing a check.

---

## Q0. Overview: already launched (2025–Oct 2026) vs. upcoming (Nov 2026–2028)

### Takeaway
Most of the relevant D/E-segment products are already launched or launching right now:
- A6 e-tron and A6 TFSI/TDI (C9)
- Q6 e-tron
- New Q7 (3rd generation)
- Q9 (deliveries from Nov 2026)
- Porsche Cayenne Electric
- Škoda Peaq

The truly new D/E EVs inside the window are few and mostly reported, not official:
- Electric Q7 (~2028, PRESS)
- RS 6 Avant (2027, SPY; EV vs PHEV unclear)
- Combustion/hybrid Macan successor (2028, PRESS)
- Electric A4 (2028–29, PRESS/SPEC)

The A8 successor and all SSP models fall outside the window, at 2029–2030 or later.

## Q1. Audi: A6/A7, A8/Landjet, Q6, Q8 e-tron successor, Q7/Q9, A4 e-tron, SSP

### Takeaway
- Audi's D/E-segment portfolio for 2026–2028 is mostly settled: the PPE-based A6 e-tron and Q6 e-tron, plus the combustion/PHEV C9 A6 and the new Q7/Q9 on the combustion platform.
- Reported new EVs inside the window: a standalone electric Q7 around 2028 and an electric A4 around 2028–2029. Both are press reports, not official.

## Q2. Audi Hungaria Győr: current and future models

### Takeaway
- Győr does **not** assemble any D/E-segment car, and no new Győr-assembled EV is announced.
- Győr builds the Q3 / Q3 Sportback (incl. e-hybrid PHEV) and the Cupra Terramar.
- It also makes the e-motors and drives for the PPE cars the company might buy (A6 e-tron, Q6 e-tron, Macan Electric), plus six-cylinder engines.
- From mid-2026, Győr also supplies Q3 bodies for joint assembly in Ingolstadt.

### Cited Findings
- **Audi Hungaria fact sheet (March 2026), 2025 figures:**
  - 200,756 cars (record) and 1,585,290 drive systems
  - Models: Q3 SUV, Q3 Sportback, Q3 SUV e-hybrid, Q3 Sportback e-hybrid; 80,669 Q3 SUV and 48,277 Sportback
  - 8,568 PHEVs
  - 71,810 Cupra Terramar (in production since Sep 2024)
  - [Audi in Ungarn 2025 PDF, March 2026](https://uploads.audi-mediacenter.com/system/production/uploaded_files/36735/file/8104327669e155b1d2684e5e0b1a695d2e041799/260317_Audi_in_Ungarn_2025_EN.pdf?1773650472)
- **Engines and drives (same PDF):**
  - 195,850 six-cylinder gasoline and 60,551 six-cylinder diesel engines
  - 264,871 electric axle drives (17% of engines)
  - PPE drives are produced at 120–280 kW. Győr electric motors are installed in the Q8 e-tron, Q6 e-tron, A6 e-tron and Porsche Macan.
  - A new manufacturing area for MEBeco drives (motor sheet-metal package, rotors and power electronics in-house) — [same PDF](https://uploads.audi-mediacenter.com/system/production/uploaded_files/36735/file/8104327669e155b1d2684e5e0b1a695d2e041799/260317_Audi_in_Ungarn_2025_EN.pdf?1773650472)
- **MEBeco series production** was expected in H1 2026 — [doing-business-in-hungary, Dec 2025](https://doing-business-in-hungary.com/?p=11357)
- **Joint Q3 production (mid-2026):** Győr builds the bodies, which go by rail to Ingolstadt for paint and assembly, under the March 2025 "agreement for the future." No other Győr plans are stated — [The EV Report, 2026-04-24](https://theevreport.com/audi-confirms-fall-2026-a2-e-tron-start-as-ingolstadt-sheds-q2)
- **Exclusive series:** Győr makes the aluminium body parts for the e-tron GT and all RS models (same PDF).

### Inferences
- **Local relevance:** for a Győr-area buyer, the local link is the drivetrain. A6 e-tron and Q6 e-tron e-motors, and likely V6 TDI/TFSI engines for the A6/Q7/Q9, are made in Győr. The cars themselves are not.
- **Whether Győr gets an EV:** the MEBeco drive investment suggests a supply role for the MEB+ cars (ID.7, Enyaq, Elroq, Peaq), not vehicle assembly.

### Gaps
- No confirmed new vehicle allocation for Győr for 2027–2028, EV or ICE, was found.
- The engine type (V6 TDI) in the Q7/Q9 was not explicitly linked to Győr in the sources.

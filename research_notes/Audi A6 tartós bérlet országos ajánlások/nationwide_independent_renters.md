# Independent (non-international-chain) renters in Hungary offering ~12-month rental of Audi A6-class cars

Research date: 2026-10-29. Method: about 20 web searches and page fetches, run in standard and extended mode. Nearly all prices come from undated web price lists, or from pages with only a "© 2026" footer. Treat every price as indicative and confirm it with the company. Labels used: **[verified page]** means the fact was read on the company's own page in this session. **[snippet only]** means the fact appeared only in a search-engine summary and was not confirmed on the page. **[dated]** means the page carries an old validity date.

## Q1: Which independent companies rent A6-class cars monthly or for about 12 months, and on what terms?

### Takeaway
At least 5 independent Budapest renters and 4 regional ones (Szeged, Debrecen, Pécs, Nyíregyháza, Miskolc) publish monthly rates for A6-class or near-equivalent cars. Viprent has the widest A6-class range: A6 Avant 399,900 Ft, BMW 520d 369,900 Ft and E-Class 599,900 Ft, all net per month. PerfectRent's A6 is the cheapest published monthly rate at 379,000 Ft + ÁFA. None of them publishes a dedicated 12-month price. They all say longer terms are negotiated individually, usually with a discount. Operating-lease brokers (NetLízing, NextAuto, Sajó Car Fleet) quote A6 rates of 330–360k net, but for 48–60-month terms and mostly for companies only.

### Inferences
- Among independents, short or monthly A6-class rental typically costs **370–600k Ft + ÁFA/month** in Budapest, with deposits of 400–500k Ft. That is about 470–760k Ft gross per month, or roughly 5.6–9.1 M Ft gross over 12 months. A 12-month contract should get a discount below these list rates, because every renter says long terms are priced individually.
- The cheapest published A6-class monthly rates are BMW 520d G31 and BMW 530e at Viprent (369,900 net) and Audi A6 at PerfectRent (379,000 net). Next come Viprent's A6 Avant (399,900 net) and PerfectRent's BMW 5 G30 (439,000 net, snippet only).
- For private persons over 12 months, the most promising options are PerfectRent, Viprent, Rentauto (which advertises a 12-month product for private persons) and Lackó (Szeged; it rents to private persons, but at 800k net it is expensive). Prémium Autó Magyarország and NextAuto appear to be company-only.
- Operating-lease brokers quote lower A6 rates (330–360k net), but only for 36–60-month contracts and usually not for private persons, so they are a poor fit for a 12-month private rental.

### Gaps
- No renter publishes a firm **12-month price**. All must be asked for a quote.
- **Nationwide delivery to Győr and its fee**: no source stated one. PerfectRent ("off-site handover") and Rentauto ("vidéken" option) suggest it is possible, but fees are unknown. Bullrent and Flexfleet deliver only within Budapest.
- Model years are missing for PerfectRent, Viprent and Bencsik. Viprent's deposit is not stated.
- No independent renter with A6-class cars was found in **Kecskemét, Székesfehérvár, Veszprém, Szombathely, Sopron or Győr**. Searches did not reach these cities within the tool budget; this is a gap, not proof that none exist.
- **Business Lease "Business Rent"** could not be re-verified: the search returned only foreign Business Lease entities. Business Lease is also part of an international group, so it may not count as "independent".
- **Volvo S90/V90** was not found at any independent renter.
- Not checked: hasznaltauto.hu and jofogas.hu rental listings, cylex and aranyoldalak directories, Rich Life (richlife.hu, luxury/M5), Leon Fleet (leonfleet.hu; no pages surfaced) and car-trade.hu (returned 503).
- Excluded as international chains: Hertz Hungary, Avis, Budget.

# Customer reviews, ratings and experiences: Hungarian car rental / long-term rental / flexible lease providers

Research date: 2026-10-29. Trustpilot figures were read directly from the live pages that day. **Google Maps ratings could not be retrieved directly**: Google Maps is not reachable through the tools used, and the aggregator sites that mirror Google ratings (magyarvelemeny.com, yably.hu) returned HTTP 403 when fetched. Where a Google-derived figure appears below, it comes from a search snippet or the company's own claim, and is labelled that way.

General caveat for the whole file: almost every independent review found is about **short-term (daily, mostly airport) rentals by foreign tourists**. **No independent review was found** that clearly concerns a 1–12-month rental (Mini Lease, Ayvens Flex, SIXT+ in Hungary, Hertz long-term). Damage and deposit themes from short-term reviews are relevant because the same return-inspection process applies, but they are not direct evidence about long-term products.

## 1. Hertz Hungary (Mercur Rent a Car Kft.), including Hertz Győr

### Takeaway
On Trustpilot, Hertz Hungary has a very small and uniformly negative sample (6 reviews, all 1-star, all about damage charges after airport rentals). Győr-specific signals come only from rental aggregators, which put it at about 8/10. No Google Maps rating could be retrieved for Budapest HQ or for Győr.

### Cited Findings
- Trustpilot "Mercur Rent a Car Ltd – Hertz International Franchisee" (hertz.hu): TrustScore 2.3 ("Poor"), **6 reviews, 100% 1-star**, only 2 in the last 12 months. The profile is unclaimed and the company does not invite reviews — [Trustpilot hertz.hu](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hertz.hu)
- All 6 reviews (Feb 2024 – Aug 2026) dispute **damage charges after return**:
  - about €1,000 for a headlight (Aug 2026, airport, booked via Expedia/Thrifty);
  - €538 for damage not visible at handover (Nov 2025, Budapest Airport);
  - €488 damage plus an out-of-hours fee, a fuel refill charge and an unexplained €199 item (Sep 2025);
  - £430 for a hairline scratch, billed after the car was cleaned (Jul 2025, BUD);
  - €438 for a small rear-bumper scratch, billed six days later (Oct 2024, BUD);
  - windscreen and boot-plastic claim with no inspection (Feb 2024).
  
  Three reviewers also describe complaint handling as slow or dismissive. None of the reviews mention long-term rental or Győr — [Trustpilot hertz.hu](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hertz.hu)
- Hertz Győr on carngo.com (aggregator): about 8/10 from "500+ reviews", with sub-scores of 7.5 for value for money and 7.9 for cleanliness. One quoted review praises fast changes to a reservation. This is an aggregator figure; it is unclear whether it covers only Győr — [Carngo Hertz Győr](https://www.carngo.com/autoberles/hungary-gyor-hertz)
- Booking.com shows 8.3/10 from 1,186 reviews for Hertz across Hungary. Skyscanner shows 4.6/5, but from only 8 reviews — [Booking.com Hertz HU](https://www.booking.com/cars/hertz/country/hu.html); [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.hu/autokolcsonzes/szolgaltato-orszag/hertz/magyarorszag/672/29475257)
- The Győr branch address differs between listings: Tompa u. 2 on one, Puskás Tivadar u. 9 on HVG Klubkártya (phone 96‑524‑529, weekdays 8–17) — [HVG Klubkártya](https://hvgklubkartya.hu/elfogadohely/hertz-autokolcsonzo-5/); [Bercode](https://www.bercode.com/shopfinder/details/11096)
- Hertz Hungary lists long-term rental (tartós bérlet) among its services, but no reviews of that product were found — [autoberlesonline.hu](https://www.autoberlesonline.hu/partnerek/hertz-autokolcsonzo/)

### Inferences
- The Trustpilot sample is tiny and self-selected (unhappy airport tourists), so it says little about the overall level of service. However, the consistency of the complaints (charges after return, often days later, without a joint inspection) is a pattern worth guarding against in a long-term contract: insist on a joint, signed return report with photos.
- Booking-platform scores (8+/10) and Trustpilot scores differ sharply. This is typical: booking platforms survey everyone who rented, while Trustpilot draws mainly complainers.

### Gaps
- Google Maps rating and review count for Hertz Budapest (HQ or Váci út / airport) and for Hertz Győr: not retrievable.
- No Hungarian forum posts (Totalcar, Index, prohardver, Reddit) on Hertz long-term rental were found.

## 2. SIXT Hungary and SIXT+ car subscription

### Takeaway
SIXT Magyarország rates poorly on Trustpilot (2.6/5 from 29 reviews, 59% 1-star). Complaints centre on damage charges for minor scratches, refuelling fees and extras added at the counter. SIXT+ subscription reviews exist only for other countries; none were found for the Hungarian product.

### Cited Findings
- Trustpilot SIXT Magyarország (sixt.hu): **TrustScore 2.6, 29 reviews (10 in the last 12 months)**. Distribution: 5★ 31%, 4★ 3%, 2★ 7%, 1★ 59%. Trustpilot notes that the company does not reply to negative reviews — [Trustpilot sixt.hu](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sixt.hu)
- Complaints in 2024–2026 by type:
  - **Fees:** extras plus a one-way fee charged for a same-location return (Sep 2026); €183 refuelling charge for 35 litres (Jul 2026); refuelling charge although the car was received only 7/8 full (Jun 2024).
  - **Car condition:** warning light at Budapest Airport, about a 6-hour wait for a replacement, no compensation (Sep 2026).
  - **Extras added without consent:** €27.94 tyre/windscreen protection (Sep 2025, Budapest).
  - **Damage charges:** about £400 for an alloy-wheel scratch billed after return (May 2025, Debrecen); €850 for a rim scratch based on a contract price list (Feb 2024).
  
  Source: [Trustpilot sixt.hu](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sixt.hu)
- Positive reviews (Aug 2026, Nov 2025) praise clear damage documentation, no surprise charges and helpful staff in Budapest — [Trustpilot sixt.hu](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sixt.hu)
- No sixt.hu review mentions SIXT+ or long-term rental — [Trustpilot sixt.hu](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sixt.hu)
- SIXT+ ratings in other countries: Italy about 4.1/5 (14 reviews), Switzerland about 3.6/5 (15 reviews), Germany about 3.3/5 (from search snippets).
  - Positive themes: transparency, same-day car swap after a breakdown, easy app.
  - Negative example: a 1-star review describes a subscription started in November 2024 that became costly, with more than $1,000 in charges and tyre-change problems.
  
  Sources: [Trustpilot SIXT+ sixt.com/plus](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sixt.com/plus); [Trustpilot SIXT+ IT](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/sixt.it/plus); [Trustpilot SIXT+ CH](https://ch.trustpilot.com/review/sixt.com/plus)

### Inferences
- For a SIXT+ or long-term SIXT contract, the risk areas suggested by short-term reviews are: price-list-based damage charges (rather than invoiced repairs), extras added at the counter, and refuelling fees. Read the contract line by line and decline unrequested extras.

### Gaps
- Google Maps rating for SIXT Budapest branches: not retrievable.
- No Hungarian-language user reports on SIXT+ were found.

## 9. Consumer protection, regulators, press and forums (cross-provider)

### Takeaway
No GVH or consumer-protection authority decision against any of the named rental companies was found. The Budapest Conciliation Body (Budapesti Békéltető Testület) publishes guidance specifically on disputed rental-car damage bills and deposit deductions. This confirms that excessive post-return damage charges are a recognised dispute category in Hungary, though no named-company cases were found.

### Cited Findings
- **BKIK Budapest Conciliation Body blog post** (undated; images suggest about June 2026):
  - Typical disputes: deposit deducted without justification; repair invoices without itemisation; unclear photos or no proof of repair; full part replacement or repainting for minor damage; costs far above an independent workshop's quote; added admin or depreciation fees.
  - Example: HUF 300,000 demanded for a small scratch where an independent quote was a fraction of that.
  - The procedure is free, can be filed online, and requires a prior written complaint to the company. A binding decision can currently be issued up to HUF 250,000.
  - No companies are named.
  
  Source: [bekeltet.bkik.hu](https://bekeltet.bkik.hu/blog/serult-berauto-tul-magas-javitasi-szamla-segitunk)
- No GVH or authority case on rental-car damage fees was found. The GVH cases that surfaced concern other markets, such as the HUF 550+ million fine on Hasznaltauto.hu's operator in October 2025 — [Telex 2025-10-30](https://telex.hu/gazdasag/2025/10/30/tobb-mint-felmilliard-forintos-birsag-hasznaltauto-hu-gvh)
- The EU-level 2019 commitment by Avis, Europcar, Enterprise, Hertz and Sixt to price transparency is described above — [economx 2019](https://www.economx.hu/auto/2019/03/25/avis-europcar-enterprise-hertz-sixt-autokolcsonzes-681100/)
- Recent press:
  - economx (2026-10-18): a Hungarian traveller recovered about €300 charged at an Italian airport rental via a bank chargeback (not a Hungarian provider);
  - beol.hu (Oct 2026): fraud charges against a man who rented cars and did not return them (not a provider issue).
  
  Sources: [economx](https://www.economx.hu/auto/2026/10/18/olaszorszag-repteri-autoberles-bankkartya-trukk/); [beol.hu](https://www.beol.hu/helyi-kek-hirek/2026/10/csalas-autoberles-gepkocsi)
- Deposit levels (general Hungarian guide, 2025): typically €500–1,200 card hold, depending on vehicle and provider; the damage excess is often €1,000–2,000 — [utazgatok.hu](https://utazgatok.hu/utazasi-tippek-tanacsok/autoberles-tanacsok-tapasztalatok/)
- **Forums and social media:** Searches for Reddit r/hungary and r/budapest, Totalcar/Index forum, prohardver/logout and Facebook groups returned **no relevant threads** about monthly or long-term rentals with any named provider. Search returned unrelated pages (e.g. Airbnb).

### Inferences
- Across sources, the dominant risk with large brands in Hungary is **post-return damage charging** (price-list-based, applied to the card days after return, without a joint inspection). Deposit-refund delays and hidden fees (refuelling, out-of-hours, extras) are secondary. For a long-term Audi A6-class rental, the mitigations are: a joint signed return protocol, photo or video evidence at both ends, an itemised invoice for any repair, and the BKIK conciliation route if disputed.
- Local Győr providers (CarMen Rent, EURO-RENT) show uniformly positive small-sample reviews. Large brands show negative Trustpilot skew driven by tourist airport rentals. Long-term customers are a different segment, and no direct evidence exists either way.

### Gaps
- Békéltető Testület decisions naming specific rental companies: none found. The decisions database was not searched directly.
- Fogyasztóvédelmi hatóság (fogyasztovedelem.kormany.hu) actions against the named companies: none found.
- Hungarian forum and Reddit experiences with long-term rental: none found despite several queries.
- Google Maps ratings for all national providers' HQs and Győr branches: not retrievable with the available tools. They should be checked manually.

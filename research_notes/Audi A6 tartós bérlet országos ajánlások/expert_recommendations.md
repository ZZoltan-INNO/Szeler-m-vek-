# Expert and media recommendations on long-term car rental in Hungary (12-month premium car, Audi A6 class), and industry recognition of providers

Research date: 2026-10-29. Method: about 20 web searches and fetches. Several key pages (hrportal.hu, arval.hu, filter.hu) returned HTTP 403, so some figures below come from search-result summaries rather than full-page reads. These are marked "(snippet-level)". The report writer should treat them as lower-confidence.

Overall caveat: independent Hungarian consumer or financial media coverage of *private-person* long-term rental (tartós bérlet) is thin. Most content on the topic is provider marketing. I found no independent comparison test that ranks monthly rental or subscription offers.

## Q1. Which providers lead the Hungarian operative lease / fleet management market, and who leads flexible or mid-term rental?

### Takeaway
Ayvens Magyarország (ALD Automotive and LeasePlan merged, rebranded September 2024) is the clear market leader, with about 33,000 managed vehicles and 2024 revenue of about HUF 89 bn. Arval is a distant second (about HUF 23.3 bn revenue in 2024). Arval completed its acquisition of Athlon (the former Mercedes-Benz fleet subsidiary) on 31 July 2026, so Arval and Athlon now form one group. Ayvens Flex and Arval Flex are the flagship "flexi" mid-term products from the big two.

### Cited Findings
- **Ayvens is the largest fleet manager in Hungary.** It serves "közel 3000 ügyfél" (nearly 3,000 clients) and manages a fleet of "mintegy 33 000 jármű" (about 33,000 vehicles). Since 18 September 2024 the former ALD Automotive and LeasePlan business has operated under the Ayvens name. — [HR Portál, 2024-09-18](https://www.hrportal.hu/c/matol-magyarorszagon-is-ayvens-neven-mukodik-az-ald-automotiveleaseplan-a-legnagyobb-hazai-flottakezelo-20240918.html); [Prim hírek](https://hirek.prim.hu/cikk/158651/); [autopro.hu](https://autopro.hu/szolgaltatok/ayvens-neven-folytatja-a-legnagyobb-hazai-flottakezelo/1219780)
- **Revenue ranking (Budapest Business Journal Book of Lists, as reported by HR Portál on 2026-03-10).** Ayvens Magyarország ranks first with 2024 revenue of about HUF 89 bn. Arval Magyarország Járműparkkezelő Kft. ranks second with about HUF 23.3 bn, which is +30% (about HUF 5 bn) on 2023 and came with a HUF 661 m loss in 2024. This is a revenue ranking for 2024, not fleet size, and not 2025 data. - **Size of the whole fleet segment (Magyar Lízingszövetség, full-year 2025).** The fleet segment's financed amount rose 16% to HUF 122.5 bn, and new contracts grew 3.6%. - **Arval hired an Ayvens executive to lead after the Athlon deal (October 2026).** This is at group level. — [newmobility.news, 2026-10-06](https://newmobility.news/en/2026/10/06/arval-poaches-ayvens-executive-to-lead-post-athlon-challenge/)
- **Flexible and mid-term products:**
  - **Ayvens Flex:** 1 to 24 months, fixed prices, no early-termination fee (according to Ayvens). Ayvens says it is "enlarging its Ayvens Flex fleet" because short-term demand has grown since COVID. — [Ayvens short-term rental](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/flottakezeles/szolgaltatasaink/short-term-rental/); [Autónavigátor, 2025-06-29](https://www.autonavigator.hu/cikkek/mi-var-a-flottapiacra-iden/)

  - **Hertz Hungary:** advertises long- and short-term rental. — [hertzhungary.hu](https://hertzhungary.hu/)

### Inferences
- For a 12-month premium car need, the large fleet managers (Ayvens, Arval/Athlon) are the most established counterparties. Their flex products (1–24 months) fit a 12-month horizon better than classic 36–48-month operative leases.
- Market-share "rankings" in the Hungarian press are revenue rankings from the BBJ Book of Lists. No public fleet-size league table for 2025–2026 was found.
- Arval and Athlon will likely operate as one group in Hungary from 2027. Anyone quoting with Athlon Hungary in late 2026 should ask about contract continuity.

### Gaps
- No 2025 or 2026 fleet-size ranking for Hungarian fleet managers is publicly available. The BBJ Book of Lists is paywalled, and both hrportal pages returned 403.
- No local figures for Athlon Hungary, Business Lease Hungary, Porsche Lízing / Porsche Mobility, MBH Lízing or Merkantil fleet volumes.
- No market-share data for mid-term or flex rental (Hertz, Europcar, SIXT, Budget, Ayvens Flex, Arval Flex).

## Q3. What do media and experts say about private persons (magánszemély) using tartós bérlet? Pros and cons, typical 2025–2026 prices, and trends

### Takeaway
Expert and media voices frame tartós bérlet and operative lease mainly as a company product, because the VAT and cost-accounting benefits only apply to businesses. Private demand is a small and shrinking share of leasing overall: households made up 8% of financed amounts in 2025, and private contracts fell 12%. Flexible short and mid-term rental is the growth area. Ayvens expected monthly fees to keep rising in 2025–2026 because of taxes, the HUF exchange rate and inflation.

### Cited Findings
**Who the product suits**
- **Road Capital:** tartós bérlet is designed for company fleets and has not spread among private persons, mainly because of tax and cost-accounting benefits. — [Road Capital](https://road-capital.com/1579-2autolizing-maganszemelyeknek-autolizing-elonyok-es-hatranyok-tartos-berlet-vs-lizing-autolizing-mukodese-havi-autolizing-dijak-autovasarlas-vagy-lizing-ceges-autoberles-vs-lizing-autolizing-fu/)
- **Filter.hu:** closed-end leasing is more favourable for private persons, while tartós bérlet suits businesses. (snippet-level, page 403) — [Filter.hu](https://www.filter.hu/cikkek/3213/autolizing-vagy-tartos-berlet-melyik-eri-meg-jobban)
- **VAT relief for companies only:** without a trip log, 50% of the VAT on operative lease fees can be reclaimed as a flat rate. The rate can be higher with a trip log. This option was extended by three years, as reported in 2025. — [Roadrecord](https://www.roadrecord.hu/utnyilvantartas-ber-es-lizing-afa-visszaigenyleshez/operativ-lizing-afa/); [Autónavigátor, 2025-06-29](https://www.autonavigator.hu/cikkek/mi-var-a-flottapiacra-iden/)

**Private persons in the leasing market (Magyar Lízingszövetség)**
- **Full-year 2025:** households made up only 8% of the financed amount and stagnated. In the retail car segment, private customers were about a quarter and businesses three quarters. Private contracts fell 12% to 14,198, and private financed volume fell 4% to HUF 67 bn. Secretary-General Kőszegi László said corporate uncertainty remains significant and large companies stayed more active. — [VG.hu, 2026-02-24](https://www.vg.hu/vilaggazdasag-magyar-gazdasag/2026/02/lizing-lizingpiac-szemelyauto-kishaszonjarmu)
- **H1 2026:** new financing rose 8.7% to HUF 519.7 bn across 42,536 contracts (+5.3%). Corporate clients were 90% of new financing, and the retail segment was HUF 45 bn. — [ado.hu](https://ado.hu/cegvilag/magyar-lizingszovetseg-az-elso-felevben-tovabb-bovult-a-magyar-lizingpiac/); [economx, 2026-08-05](https://www.economx.hu/magyar-gazdasag/2026/08/05/magyar-lizingpiac-finanszirozas-lizingszovetseg-novekedes/)
- **HVG:** after a good year, the leasing market was in "wait-and-see" mode until the April 2026 elections (headline only, not read in full). — [HVG, 2026-02-24](https://hvg.hu/gazdasag/20260224_lizing-lizingszovetseg-auto-kamat-valasztas-agardi-marianna)

**Market trends and expert views (Hegedűs Viktor, Ayvens, June 2025)**
- Average lease terms are now "50 hónap felett" (above 50 months), up from 42–46 months before COVID, and companies are extending existing contracts.
- Demand for short-term rental has grown since COVID, and Ayvens is expanding Ayvens Flex.
- Further increases in prices and monthly fees are expected because of tax increases (company car tax up from 1 January 2025; plug-in hybrids registered in 2025 are no longer exempt), HUF exchange-rate swings, inflation and tariffs.
- Companies are shifting to smaller, lower-spec cars.
- Source for all four points: [Autónavigátor, 2025-06-29](https://www.autonavigator.hu/cikkek/mi-var-a-flottapiacra-iden/). This is dated mid-2025, and it is an interview with a provider.

**Ayvens' own "5+1 myths" piece (published 2025-01-29, updated 2026-02-26)**
- It argues that customers should compare total cost of ownership (TCO), not purchase price against the sum of the fees.
- The fixed monthly fee includes service, maintenance, taxes, insurance, 0–24 assistance and a replacement car.
- The lessor bears the residual-value risk.
- Flex rental is available from 1 month with no fixed term.

### Inferences
- **Rough price band.** Based on the BMW X6, GLC and Superb data points, an Audi A6-class car on a 12-month flexible rental in Hungary is likely to cost roughly HUF 400,000–600,000 + ÁFA per month (about HUF 510–760k gross) with insurance and service included. This is an inference, not a quoted price. A classic 36–48-month operative lease on the same car would be noticeably cheaper per month, but it locks the customer in.
- **Price versus buying for one year.** For a private person, ÁFA is not reclaimable and the company car tax is built into the fee. A 12-month flex rental of an A6 therefore likely costs HUF 6–9 m gross in total. Buying a used A6 for a year and reselling it typically costs depreciation plus running costs, often well below that total, but it carries resale, repair and time risk. Experts generally say rental "pays off" for privates only when flexibility, no capital lock-up and no risk are worth the premium.
- **Value for companies and sole traders.** For a company or sole trader (egyéni vállalkozó), the 50% flat-rate VAT recovery and full cost deductibility make operative lease and tartós bérlet considerably more attractive than for a private person.

### Gaps
- I found no independent 2025–2026 article (totalcar, vezess, pénzcentrum, bankmonitor, privátbankár, portfolio) that calculates rental against buying against leasing for a private person. Several targeted searches returned only provider pages.
- I found no published Audi A6 monthly rental price from a major provider. Quotes must be requested directly.
- I found no independent statistic on the share of private persons among tartós bérlet or flex customers.

## Q4. Are there comparison articles of autó-előfizetés or havi bérlés offers with prices, and what should a quote checklist include?

### Takeaway
I found no Hungarian comparative test or ranking of monthly rental or subscription offers from the requested outlets. The practical checklist below is assembled from fleet trade content and provider terms. It is general guidance distilled from the sources, not a single expert's list.

### Cited Findings
- **No comparison article found.** Searches for "autó-előfizetés … összehasonlítás" returned provider pages (SIXT+, Swapp, MOL Limo car-sharing at HUF 1,490/month for a 10% discount, which is not a subscription) and an unrelated Pénzcentrum article on compulsory insurance (KGFB) prices (2026-10-27). — [SIXTplus](https://sixtplus.hu/); [MOL Limo](https://mollimo.hu/); [Pénzcentrum, 2026-10-27](https://www.penzcentrum.hu/auto/20261027/sulyos-tizezreket-fizetnek-a-magyar-autosok-a-biztositasra-kiderult-melyik-markakra-a-legolcsobb-a-kotelezo-1207937)
- **Kilometre allowance.** Contracts set an annual km allowance, typically 15,000–30,000 km per year, and excess km is charged. — [Roadrecord / search summary](https://www.roadrecord.hu/utnyilvantartas-ber-es-lizing-afa-visszaigenyleshez/operativ-lizing-afa/); [Netrisk blog](https://www.netrisk.hu/blog/bank/auto-lizing--elonyok--hatranyok--buktatok--feltetelek--mire-figyeljunk-oda-)
- **Down payment.** Down payment or önrész requirements vary from 0% to 50% depending on the provider and product, and some require 25% from private persons. — [search summary across provider pages](https://kgydauto.hu/tartos-berlet/)
- **Deposits.** Deposits (kaució) of HUF 100–300k appear in 12-month offers. — [cegesautoberles.hu](https://cegesautoberles.hu/auto-tartos-berlet-arak)
- **Notice and early termination.** Ayvens Flex advertises no early-termination fee. Swapp requires 14 days' notice. — [Ayvens](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/flottakezeles/szolgaltatasaink/short-term-rental/); [Az Utazó](https://azutazo.hu/elofizetes-autora-becs-utan-budapesten-is-elerheto-a-swapp/)
- **What the fee includes.** The scope differs between providers. Typical components are KGFB, casco, service, tyres, assistance, a replacement car, and registration and company car tax. — [Ayvens 5+1](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/rolunk/hirek/flottakezeles/tevhitek-az-operativ-lizingrol/); [SIXTplus](https://sixtplus.hu/)

### Checklist for quotes
This is synthesised from the sources above. It is an inference, not from one source.
1. Is the price net or gross (+ÁFA)? Is it available to a private person at all, or only to companies?
2. What is the annual or monthly km allowance, and what is the price per excess km? Is there a refund for unused km?
3. What insurance is included? Check casco and its deductible (önrész), KGFB, glass, and theft cover. Is there cross-border use and a green card?
4. Are service, tyres (winter and summer, storage), assistance and a replacement car included, and within what time limit?
5. Is company car tax (cégautóadó), registration tax or weight tax included or passed through?
6. Down payment, deposit, and when and how the deposit is returned.
7. What are the minimum term, the notice period and the early-termination fee?
8. What are the return-inspection rules ("fair wear and tear" catalogue, damage pricing)? Can an independent assessment be requested?
9. Is the car exactly the one quoted (model, engine, equipment) or "or similar class"? This is common in flex and rental products.
10. How is the price indexed? Are HUF/EUR-indexed fees possible? Ayvens warns that monthly fees may rise with exchange rates and inflation.
11. Are there credit-check requirements? Be wary of "no credit check" or "BAR listás is" offers from small operators.

### Inferences
- Since no media comparison exists, a buyer should request at least three written quotes on the same specification (car class, 12 months, same km allowance, same insurance deductible) from a large fleet manager (Ayvens Flex or Arval Flex), a rental major (Hertz, SIXT+ or Europcar), and a premium or dealer-group provider (for example Porsche Mobility, which sells Audi).

- I did not verify current prices for Europcar, Budget or Porsche Mobility/Porsche Lízing in Hungary, or Audi-specific offers.

# Hungarian leasing companies, bank lessors and brand finance arms: 12-month operative lease for Audi A6-class cars (research date 2026-10-29)

Scope: Audi A6 / A6 Avant / A6 e-tron, BMW 5 Series / i5, Mercedes E-Class / EQE, Volvo S90/V90. Providers named in the brief, plus Ayvens Flex follow-up and the 2027 cégautóadó.

Method note: web search was a US-based index and returned almost nothing Hungarian for these providers. Most findings come from fetching the providers' own websites directly. Several sites could not be reached from this environment: arval.hu, mbhbank.hu, netlizing.hu (HTTP 403 / Cloudflare), mobilease.hu, mbhlizing.hu (proxy refused), merkantil.hu (503), unicreditleasing.hu (TLS error), mercedes-benz.hu, volvocars.com, bmw.hu (403 / no answer). For these the gaps are a matter of access, not proof that the product does not exist.

## Q1. Which providers offer a 12-month (or shorter) term, and what is the minimum term?

### Takeaway
Of the providers checked, only three publish a term of 12 months or less: Ayvens Flex (1–24 months, BMW 5 class listed), Business Lease "Business Rent" (2 weeks to 1 year, but only up to VW Passat class) and Flexfleet (1 to 12+ months, Škoda Superb class). The bank lessors (MBH, Raiffeisen, UniCredit, Merkantil/OTP) and the brand finance arms (Porsche Finance, Kinto, BMW, Mercedes, Volvo) publish no 12-month operative lease for A6-class cars. Kinto states 24–60 months. A 12-month term has to be negotiated with these providers individually.

### Inferences
- For an A6-class car on a true 12-month term, Ayvens Flex is the only verified published option among the providers checked. Its Exclusive class is BMW 5 / X5, so an Audi A6 or Mercedes E is not guaranteed. Ayvens says model availability within a category is confirmed only after a quote request.
- Business Rent and Flexfleet cover 12 months or less, but their published fleets stop at Passat/Superb class. A premium car would need a special request.
- Bank lessors (Raiffeisen, UniCredit, MBH, Merkantil) appear to focus on financial lease and long-term (36–60 month) operative lease for companies. A 12-month term would be an individual negotiation, probably expensive because of residual-value risk (inference).

### Gaps
- Arval Hungary, MBH Lízing, NetLízing and Mobilease: sites blocked. Their minimum terms and mid-term rental products could not be verified. Phone them or check the sites from a normal browser.
- Athlon: athlon.com/hu returns 404. Athlon works in Hungary only through Business Lease, so there is no separate "Athlon Hungary" product.
- Porsche Finance Autoabo (Hungary): page content did not render. Terms, models and prices are unknown.

## Q2. Availability to private persons (magánszemély) vs companies

### Takeaway
None of the providers checked publicly confirms a 12-month operative lease for private persons. Ayvens Flex, Business Rent, Flexfleet, NextAuto, Sajó Car Fleet and Kinto are all written for businesses ("céges autó", "vállalkozásának"). Flexfleet's form at least asks whether the requester is a private person or a company. EasyLease does not restrict renter type and advertises "no credit check".

### Cited Findings
- Ayvens Flex: "Az Ayvens Flex-el rugalmas feltételekkel bérelhet céges autót rövid távra" and "Tartsa mozgásban vállalkozását!". Every use case is corporate (waiting for a new car, a new business line, new employees, expats). Neither egyéni vállalkozó nor magánszemély is mentioned. — [Ayvens Flex offers page](https://autotartosberlet.ayvens.com/rovidtavu-autoberles); [Ayvens Flex HU](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/flottakezeles/szolgaltatasaink/short-term-rental/)
- Business Rent: described as an agreement between two companies; only company renters are mentioned. — [Business Lease – Cégautó bérlés](https://www.businesslease.hu/termekek-es-szolgaltatasok/cegauto-berles)
- Flexfleet: tagline "Teljes körű flottakezelés cégeknek"; the contact form asks "magánszemély vagy cég". — [flexfleet.hu](https://flexfleet.hu/)
- NextAuto: "Flottakezelés és tartós bérlet vállalkozásoknak", including KKV packages. — [nextauto.hu](https://www.nextauto.hu/)
- Sajó Car Fleet: "Autót adunk vállalkozásához". — [sajocarfleet.hu](https://www.sajocarfleet.hu/)
- Kinto Hungary: written for "cégautó" / "vállalkozása". — [KINTO Hungary](https://www.kinto-mobility.eu/hu/hu)
- Kinto One Lexus campaign (autumn 2025) was "a magyar flottavásárlók számára" (for Hungarian fleet buyers); private buyers are not explicitly excluded. — [Autonavigator, 2025-10-09](https://www.autonavigator.hu/cikkek/ilyen-eddig-meg-nem-volt-a-lexusnal/)
- EasyLease: "nincs hitelbírálat" (no credit check), up to 5 users, new drivers allowed; individual offers on request. Renter type is not restricted, though the page stresses cost deductibility and partial VAT reclaim, which points to business users. — [easylease.hu](https://www.easylease.hu/)
- Porsche Finance: its products serve both lakossági (retail) and corporate customers in general. Operative-lease eligibility is not specified. — [porschefinance.hu](https://www.porschefinance.hu/)
- Raiffeisen Lízing passenger-car financing is labelled "Céges" (corporate). — [lizing.raiffeisen.hu](https://lizing.raiffeisen.hu/)

### Inferences
- An egyéni vállalkozó (sole trader, with a tax number) is legally a business, so it is probably eligible for Ayvens Flex and Business Rent, subject to credit check. Neither provider confirms this publicly, so ask explicitly.
- A pure private person will probably have to use a rent-a-car monthly rate (e.g. Europcar/Eurent via Porsche Finance Group, Sixt via AutoWallis) or a private-lease specialist rather than these B2B lessors.

### Gaps
- Ayvens: egyéni vállalkozó eligibility is not published.
- Arval, MBH, NetLízing and Mobilease private-lease offers: sites blocked, so not verified.

- For an Audi A6 specifically, the realistic routes are an Ayvens Flex Exclusive request asking for an A6, an individual quote from Porsche Finance (Audi's captive) or Audi dealers, or Business Lease/Merkantil on request. None of these publishes a price.

## Q6. 2027 cégautóadó (company car tax) rates

### Takeaway
NAV published the indexed 2027 rates on 2026-10-19 (Ügyféliránytű). For a petrol or diesel A6-class car above 120 kW in environmental class "6–10" (Euro 5/6), the rate is 77,000 Ft/month in 2027, up from 76,000 in 2026. Separately, the October 2026 government tax bill would split the over-120 kW band into four from 2027, with much higher rates. That bill is not yet law.

### Cited Findings
- NAV "Cégautóadó 2027. évre valorizált mértéke" (page dated 2026.10.19), Ft/month per car:

| kW | "0–4" | "6–10" | "5"; "14–15" |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–50 | 39,000 | 20,000 | 17,500 |
| 51–90 | 51,500 | 25,500 | 20,000 |
| 91–120 | 77,000 | 51,500 | 25,500 |
| over 120 | 102,000 | 77,000 | 51,500 |

  — [NAV – Cégautóadó 2027](https://nav.gov.hu/ugyfeliranytu/adokulcsok_jarulekmertekek/valorizalt-adomertekek/cegautoado/cegautoado-2027)
- 2026 rates for comparison (published 2025-10-27): over 120 kW 101,000 / 76,000 / 51,000. — [megkimelt.hu summary citing NAV](https://megkimelt.hu/kalkulatorok/cegautoado); [NAV – Cégautóadó 2026](https://nav.gov.hu/ugyfeliranytu/adokulcsok_jarulekmertekek/valorizalt-adomertekek/cegautoado/cegautoado)
- Mechanism: rates are indexed by the July CPI, rounded to 500 or 1,000 Ft, and published by NAV by 31 October of the preceding year. — [megkimelt.hu](https://megkimelt.hu/kalkulatorok/cegautoado)
- **Pending bill (törvényjavaslat, Oct 2026 tax package)**: the over-120 kW band would be split into 121–140 / 141–180 / 181–250 / >250 kW.

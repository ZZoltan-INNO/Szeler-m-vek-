# Melges 24 for sale in Italy and Croatia on pan-European / international boat portals (checked 2026-11-21)

Scope and method. I checked boat24 (EN/IT/DE/HR, plus the boot24.ch and yacht.de mirrors), iNautia, TopBoats, Apollo Duck, Band of Boats, YachtWorld/boats.com (incl. yachtworld.it), Scanboat, Listingsport, Mondialbroker, Sailboatlistings, Boat-Specs, DailyBoats, Yachtall, Boatshop24, best-boats24, Rightboat, Barcheusate and Bootsbörse. I also searched the builder names "White Formula", "Devoti", "Melges 24 OD" and "Melges Performance". Pages were opened with WebFetch and with headless Chromium (Playwright). I did not try to get around any bot protection.

Several portals block automated access, so for those I relied on search-engine snippets:
- iNautia, TopBoats, Boatshop24 and YachtWorld.it: Akamai "Access Denied" (403).
- Yachtall, DailyBoats, best-boats24 and boats.com: Cloudflare "Just a moment" (403).
- Apollo Duck: BotStopper/Anubis "Access Denied".
- Rightboat: an HTTP 202 challenge.
- Barcheusate.it and Bootsbörse: HTTP 502 errors.
- boat24 blocks curl and headless Chromium (403), but WebFetch could read it.

**Bottom line:**
- Only **one** Melges 24 physically in Italy can be verified as live on an international portal today: **boat24 751393** (1993, Lago Maggiore, €12,500). It is mirrored on boot24.ch and yacht.de.
- A **second, probably live** one is the **iNautia/TopBoats 2001 Monfalcone boat** (€24,000, private seller). It is visible only through search snippets.
- Apollo Duck **558715 "ITA 528 Rome"** is indexed but unreadable and probably stale.
- **No Melges 24 located in Croatia was found on any international portal.**

## Q1. boat24.com / boot24.ch / yacht.de: is ID 751393 a genuine Melges 24, and are there other Melges 24s in Italy or Croatia?

### Takeaway
boat24 751393 is live on 2026-11-21: 1993, Lago Maggiore, €12,500 negotiable (CHF 11,600 on boot24.ch), private seller, posted 16/11/2026. It is the only used Melges 24 in the whole boat24 database, so there is none in Croatia, Slovenia, Austria or Switzerland either. The spec fields (7.20 × 2.25 m, 500 kg ballast, roller-reefing genoa) do not match the class. The photos, however, show a Melges-branded flush-deck sportboat named "Windbuster" that looks like a genuine early Melges 24. My reading is that it is a real Melges 24 with sloppy seller-entered data, and the hull number should be confirmed.

# Garmin Varia RearVue 820: Hungarian webshop prices and baseline product facts (checked 2026-11-04)

Method note: Árukereső (arukereso.hu) and Árgép (argep.hu) returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, and so did garmin.hu (main domain) and alza.hu. For those four, prices come from search-engine index snippets, or for Garmin Hungary from its mirror page dev.garmin.hu, and are marked "snippet only / not page-verified". Every other price below comes from a live fetch of the shop's own product page on 2026-11-04. Several search snippets disagreed with the live pages. On Bringaland at least, the snippets appear to have picked up the price of the **Varia eRTL615** (105.900 Ft), which sits in the same page's related-products strip. Treat snippet prices with caution.

## Q1. Official Garmin Hungary list price and baseline product facts (to verify offers are the right product)

### Takeaway
On garmin.hu the Varia RearVue 820 (international version, part number **010-03180-00**, EAN 753759357467) lists at **111 900 Ft**, which also serves as the de facto Hungarian list price. Garmin announced it worldwide on 3 Feb 2026 at an MSRP of $299.99 / €299.99 / £259.99. A separate German **StVZO** version, **010-03180-10**, has different light specs. It should not be mixed in with offers for 010-03180-00.

### Inferences

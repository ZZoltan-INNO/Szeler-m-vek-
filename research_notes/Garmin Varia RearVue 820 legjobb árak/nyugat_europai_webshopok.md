### Inferences
- The Tradeinn price changes with the destination country. The same page showed **USD 247.99** to a US (non-EU) visitor and **97,845 Ft "VAT & duties included"** with Hungary selected. This confirms that Tradeinn charges Hungarian 27% VAT (OSS) for HU buyers, and the HUF figure is the real landed price.

The Shopify HU market price of 111,400 Ft is about €306.86, so it carries about 3.6% on top of the VAT difference (FX or market markup). My cart was not localised and showed €279.90 with free DPD shipping to Hungary. A checkout with a Hungarian address under Shopify Markets will most likely switch to the 111,400 Ft price. Expect ≈111,400 Ft, not €279.90.
- **Cicli Drigani** says that EU buyers see "the price including VAT for the respective country". The €249 I saw was the Italian-market price (22% VAT). If it is re-priced to 27% HU VAT, the estimate is €249 / 1.22 × 1.27 ≈ €259.20, plus €9.90 shipping, which is **≈€269 / ≈97,700 Ft**. That is about the same as Tradeinn. If no re-pricing happens, the total is €258.90.
- Effective ranking for a Hungarian buyer:

### Inferences

Carrier clearance fees are typically charged as well.
- Example for Tredz: £200 net ≈ €239.64, plus 27% HU VAT ≈ €304.34 before shipping and clearance. That is not better than the EU offers.

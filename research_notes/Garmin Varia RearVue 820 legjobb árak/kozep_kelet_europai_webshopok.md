- **Cheapest delivered option:** Svijet Medija with `garmin25` is the cheapest by a wide margin. Even with €20–30 EU shipping it would land at about 89,000–92,000 Ft, below Alza.hu's 111,890 Ft and below every CZ, SK or PL domestic price converted to HUF.
- **Hungarian VAT at checkout (OSS):** None of the four HR/SI shops says whether it charges Hungarian 27% VAT under OSS, so the checkout price may change. If a shop is above the €10,000 EU distance-selling threshold, the HU-VAT price would be about:
   - A2U €269.95 → €281.01
   - Maxisport €269.96 → €281.02
   - Svijet Medija €224.25 → €227.84 (with code; €299 → €303.78 without)
   - Keindl €299.99 → €304.79

  These are my own calculations (net price × 1.27). The shops' pages did not state them.
- **Keindl shipping cost:** For an order over €90 to Hungary the rule is ambiguous. The only explicit "free above €90" rule is for Croatia, so assume €20 until the cart shows otherwise.
- **Warranty:** All shops show 24-month warranty or EU statutory guarantee. A Slovenian shop (Bokal Sport) states that the Garmin manufacturer warranty "velja na geografskem območju Republike Slovenije" (applies in the territory of Slovenia). So a Hungarian buyer of a SI/HR unit should expect to make claims through the selling shop (EU 2-year seller liability), not necessarily through Garmin Hungary.

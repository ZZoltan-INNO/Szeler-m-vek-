# Operative lease, short-term lease and car subscription in Hungary for an Audi A6-class car (~12 months)

Research date: 2026-10-29. All HUF prices are net (+27% ÁFA) unless stated otherwise. Where a source is undated or older, this is flagged inline. Ayvens prices come from data embedded in the live Ayvens HU offers site (autotartosberlet.ayvens.com), retrieved on 2026-10-29. Model records there carry 2024–2026 creation dates, which suggests the data is current.

## 1. Which providers offer a 12-month (or shorter) minimum term, and on what terms?

### Takeaway
Standard Hungarian operative lease runs 24–60 months. Three routes give a ~12-month car: (a) **Ayvens Flex**, the clearest option. It is a full-service company rental from 1 month up to 24 months with penalty-free return after month 1, and it has a published "Exclusive" category (BMW 5 Series / X5). (b) The **Mercedes-Benz dealer operative lease (JP Auto)**, offered "already for a 12-month period" with no down payment. (c) Short/mid-term rental firms such as Business Lease "Business Rent", Prémium Autó Magyarország (from 3 months) and PerfectRent. All of these target companies. No source confirmed private-individual eligibility for a 12-month product.

### Cited Findings

It starts "akár már 1 hónapra" (from as little as 1 month), runs "1 hónaptól 24 hónapig", and the car can be extended or returned at any time "kötbérmentesen" (without penalty). The fee includes servicing/maintenance, insurance, taxes and administration. The FAQ adds 24h assistance, a replacement car (repair >1 day) and the motorway vignette. ## 4. Electric alternatives (A6 e-tron, i5, EQE, Tesla) — tax advantages and lease prices

### Takeaway
Pure EVs (environmental class **5E**, and 5Z) are **outside the scope of cégautóadó** (company car tax). Plug-in hybrids (5P/5N) registered from 2025 are taxable, and older PHEVs lose the exemption after **2026-12-31**. For an A6 diesel (>120 kW, Euro 6, class "6–10"), the 2026 cégautóadó is **76,000 Ft/month**, so a BEV saves ~0.9 M Ft/year if the tax is not already bundled into the fee. Hungarian lease prices for the A6 e-tron, i5 and EQE were not found. The Tesla Model 3/Y and BMW i4 have published prices.

### Cited Findings
- 5E (pure electric) and 5Z cars are not subject to cégautóadó. From 2025-01-01, 5P (PHEV) and 5N (range extender) cars are taxable. For 5P/5N cars registered before 2025-01-01, the exemption continues until 2026-12-31 — [NAV: A cégautóadót érintő változások](https://nav.gov.hu/ado/egyeb/A_cegautoadot_erinto_valtozasok); see also [Roadrecord cégautóadó summary 2026-01-22 (PDF)](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- 2026 cégautóadó (HUF/month): over 120 kW is 101,000 (class 0–4), **76,000 (class 6–10)** and 51,000 (class 5, 14–15). 91–120 kW is 76,000 / 51,000 / 25,000. PHEVs registered after 2024-09-01 get no green plate, and existing PHEV green plates must be swapped by 2026-11-30. Published 2025-12-29, updated 2026-02-26 — [Ayvens: 2026 adóemelések](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/rolunk/hirek/flottakezeles/2026-adoemelesek-es-szabalyozasi-valtozasok/)
- 2026 registration tax for over 250 kW cars is 423,000 Ft. NAV publishes next year's rates by 31 October each year, so 2027 rates are due around 2026-10-31 — [Ayvens: 2026 adóemelések](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/rolunk/hirek/flottakezeles/2026-adoemelesek-es-szabalyozasi-valtozasok/)
- EV lease prices: Ayvens BMW i4 eDrive 340 HP 267,990 / 344,990 net (48 months/15k km). Volvo EX60 P6 267,990 / 341,990. Ayvens Flex Green+ (Tesla 3, BMW iX3, BYD Seal) **441,000/month**, 1–24 months — [Ayvens](https://autotartosberlet.ayvens.com/rovidtavu-autoberles)
- Flexfleet (60 months): Tesla Model 3 SR RWD 178,096 / 228,377. **Tesla Model Y Premium LR AWD 245,637 / 315,971** net — [Flexfleet](https://flexfleet.hu/tartos-berlet-ajanlataink/)
- Tesla Model Y HU list prices: Standard from 16,669,900 Ft (Oct 2025). Performance 25,369,900 Ft — [Autónavigátor](https://www.autonavigator.hu/cikkek/itt-az-uj-tesla-model-y-performance-mar-be-is-araztak-itthon/)

### Inferences
- When cégautóadó is bundled into the lease fee (as in Business Rent and Kinto One), the EV saving appears as a lower fee rather than a separate saving. For a self-arranged lease, an EV avoids ~76k/month vs. a diesel A6.
- Ayvens Flex Green+ (441k) is ~125k/month cheaper than Flex Exclusive (566k), and the EV also has no cégautóadó. For a 12-month company use case, a Tesla Model 3/BMW iX3 via Flex is the cheapest "premium-feel" option found. A Tesla Model Y on a 60-month lease (316k) is far cheaper monthly but locks in 5 years.
- A PHEV (e.g. A6 e-hybrid, E 300e, 530e) taken now would pay cégautóadó. It has no green-plate advantage if first registered after 2024-09-01.

### Gaps
- No Hungarian operative lease or Flex price found for the Audi A6 e-tron, BMW i5 or Mercedes EQE / Tesla Model S.
- 2027 cégautóadó rates (due by 2026-10-31) not yet captured.
- Claim from a search summary (not opened at source): VAT on company car lease fees remains at least 50% deductible. Verify with a tax adviser.

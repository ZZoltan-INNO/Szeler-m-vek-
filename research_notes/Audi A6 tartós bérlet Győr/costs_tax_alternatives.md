# 12-month rental of an Audi A6-class car in Hungary (near Győr): costs, tax, contract terms, alternatives

Research date: 2026-10-29. Exchange rate used for conversions: about 364 HUF/EUR (Economx quote, 15 Oct 2026; see Q5). All HUF conversions of EUR prices are my own arithmetic, not source figures.

Overall caveat: I found **no published Hungarian 12-month price for an A6-class car**. Hungarian providers quote on request. Price levels below come from German auto-subscription comparison sites and need a Hungarian quote to confirm.

## Q1. Typical contract terms in Hungarian long-term rental (km, over-km fee, deposit, excess, return rules, tyres, foreign travel, early termination)

### Takeaway
Hungarian 1–12-month rentals (for example Budget Hungary) bundle insurance, assistance, vignette, maintenance and winter tyres into one monthly fee with a monthly km cap of 2,000–4,000 km. The main extra costs are the CASCO excess (önrész), fuel and over-km charges. I found **no published HUF figures** for deposit, excess or over-km rates. These are the items to get in writing in each quote.

### Cited Findings
- Budget Hungary offers monthly rental to private and business customers for **1–12 months**, with compact, SUV and premium models. It markets this as up to 40% cheaper than daily rental (a marketing claim). The monthly fee is said to cover roadside assistance, a replacement car, maintenance and motorway vignette — [Budget HU post via LinkedIn (Harmat Eszter)](https://hu.linkedin.com/in/harmateszter)
- The same Budget offer: 12 categories, **2,000–4,000 km monthly limits**, young cars. The fee covers insurance, assistance, Hungarian vignette, maintenance and **winter tyres**. No prices are published, and the post does not say whether Audi models are offered — [Budget HU post via LinkedIn](https://hu.linkedin.com/in/harmateszter)
- In Hungarian long-term rental the monthly fee typically covers tyres, servicing, inspections, repairs from normal use, KGFB and CASCO. Extra items the renter still pays are the **excess for an insurance event (önrész), fuel, and charges when the contracted mileage is exceeded** — [adozona.hu Q&A, "Személygépjárművek tartós bérlete"](https://adozona.hu/kerdesek/2017_2_23_SZEMELYGEPJARMUVEK_TARTOS_BERLETE_fli) (2017 expert answer)
- Lexus Kinto One (Hungary, autumn 2025) is a manufacturer subscription benchmark: a **24-month** term with **0% down payment**. The monthly fee includes insurance, assistance **and the cégautóadó**, based on **15,000 km/year** — [Autonavigator](https://www.autonavigator.hu/autosvilag_hirei/ilyen-eddig-meg-nem-volt-a-lexusnal) (dated autumn 2025, so the offer may have ended)
- Benchmark from the Dutch market: a VWP Audi A6 short-lease charges **€0.19/km (excl. VAT)** for extra km. Repairs, maintenance, standard tyres and road tax are included — [vwpshortlease.nl](https://www.vwpshortlease.nl/en/aanbod/audi-a6)
- Benchmark for foreign travel, from Sixt+ Germany: countries are grouped in zones. Zone 2 limits depend on vehicle make. The customer must notify Sixt before driving abroad, and the foreign-travel add-on costs **€9.99/month**. A breach can bring a contractual penalty, and **the protection cover lapses** — [Sixt+ help center (DE)](https://www.sixt.de/plus/help-center/articles/fahrzeugnutzung/)

### Inferences
- Get each of the following in writing from each Hungarian bidder (Budget, Hertz, Avis/ Sixt HU, Ayvens/ALD, Arval, dealer-network rental via Porsche Hungaria/Audi dealers):
  - monthly km allowance and the HUF/km over-km fee (and any refund for unused km)
  - kaució amount and whether it is a card hold or a cash deposit
  - CASCO önrész (often a percentage of damage with a HUF minimum) and the price of önrész-csökkentés
  - the written return policy (visszavételi szabályzat / fair wear-and-tear catalogue, for example the maximum allowed scratch or dent size), and whether a joint inspection report is made at handover
  - who pays the cégautóadó (normally built into the fee because the rental company owns the car; see Q2)
  - foreign-travel zones (Austria is next door to Győr, so this matters)
  - early-termination formula (for example remaining months × x%)
- With a monthly cap of 2,000–4,000 km, a driver doing 30,000+ km/year needs the upper band. Otherwise over-km fees can add materially; at a Dutch-style €0.19/km, 5,000 extra km is about €950 (my arithmetic).

### Gaps
- No Hungarian provider published HUF figures for kaució, önrész, önrész-csökkentés, over-km fee or early-termination penalty. A search of Hungarian ÁSZF documents found no long-term car rental ÁSZF with these numbers.
- No Hungarian monthly price was found for an A6/5-Series/E-Class-class car on a 12-month term.

## Q2. Tax: cégautóadó 2026, who pays in a rental, ÁFA deductibility, cost deduction, SZJA on private use, EV/green-plate benefits

### Takeaway
In 2026 the cégautóadó for a >120 kW car is **101,000 / 76,000 / 51,000 Ft per month**, depending on environmental class. A Euro 6 A6 40 TDI (150 kW) most likely falls in the 51,000 Ft column. In a rental the **owner (the rental company) is the taxpayer** and prices it into the fee. A company renter can deduct **50% of the rental VAT** without a logbook; this Hungarian derogation runs to **31 Dec 2027**. A full deduction is possible only with a detailed log of business use. A private person can recover nothing. Pure EVs are outside cégautóadó entirely.

### Cited Findings
**Cégautóadó rates 2026 (monthly, per car)** — [NAV information sheet "Cégautóadó és az ahhoz kapcsolódó szja-szabályok" (published 22 Jan 2026)](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf); the same table appears in [Ayvens HU 2026 changes](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/rolunk/hirek/flottakezeles/2026-adoemelesek-es-szabalyozasi-valtozasok/) and [NAV notice HÉ 2025/49 via adozona](https://adozona.hu/jogtar/497937_NAV_kozlemeny_HE_2025_49_2026_valorizalt):

| kW | class "0"–"4" | class "6"–"10" | class "5", "14"–"15" |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–50 | 38,500 | 20,000 | 17,500 |
| 51–90 | 51,000 | 25,000 | 20,000 |
| 91–120 | 76,000 | 51,000 | 25,000 |
| >120 | **101,000** | **76,000** | **51,000** |

- The rates are indexed each year by inflation. The 2025 0–50 kW row was 37,000 / 19,000 / 17,000 Ft — [adozona search summary of NAV table](https://adozona.hu/jogtar/497937_NAV_kozlemeny_HE_2025_49_2026_valorizalt)
- The tax is declared and paid quarterly, by the 20th day of the month after the quarter. Form 2601 is used in 2026 — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- **Who pays.** NAV: "Az adó alanya hatósági nyilvántartásban szereplő személygépkocsi esetén a járműnyilvántartás szerint bejegyzett tulajdonosa, kivéve, ha a tulajdonos magánszemély és a személygépkocsi után nem számol el költséget." In English: for a registered car, the taxpayer is the registered owner, unless the owner is a private person who claims no costs. For **pénzügyi lízing** the lessee is the taxpayer, unless the lessee is a private person claiming no costs. Operative rental is not listed as shifting the tax, so the owner (the rental company) remains the taxpayer — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- An adozona expert answer agrees: when the lessor has paid cégautóadó on its own car, the lessee company has **no cégautóadó liability** (citing Gjt. 17/B § (1)) — [adozona Q&A "Bérelt személygépkocsi költség és ..."](https://adozona.hu/kerdesek/2017_1_17_Berelt_szemelygepkocsi_koltseg_es_tas) (older answer)
- Costs that count as car costs, and so trigger cégautóadó for a private-person owner who claims them, include fuel, repair and maintenance, **"a személygépkocsi bérleti-, illetve lízingdíja"** (the rental or lease fee), depreciation, KGFB, baleseti adó, CASCO and gépjárműadó — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- **Egyéni vállalkozó / private person.** Cégautóadó arises only when costs are claimed by the itemised method (tételes költségelszámolás, based on a road log). It does **not** arise when an egyéni vállalkozó claims the flat 500 km/month without proof. Nor does it arise for a private person who is reimbursed under a kiküldetési rendelvény (business-trip order), or who gets the commuting allowance of up to 30 Ft/km — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- A **foreign-registered car** also triggers cégautóadó if costs are claimed on it under the Szja tv. or the Sztv. (Hungarian accounting law) — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- Gépjárműadó paid on time can be deducted from the cégautóadó for the same months, but **only if both taxes fall on the same person** — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- **EV / PHEV.** From 1 Jan 2025 only pure electric and zero-emission cars count as környezetkímélő (environment-friendly) and are outside cégautóadó. **Plug-in hybrids (5P) and range extenders (5N) became taxable in 2025.** Those registered before 1 Jan 2025 stay exempt **until 31 Dec 2026** — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- Plug-in hybrids first registered after 1 Sept 2024 cannot get a green plate. Existing PHEV green plates must be swapped at the next technical inspection, **by 30 Nov 2026 at the latest** — [Ayvens HU](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/rolunk/hirek/flottakezeles/2026-adoemelesek-es-szabalyozasi-valtozasok/)
- **ÁFA on rental fees.** Under Áfa tv. 124. § (4) b), 50% of the input VAT on renting or leasing a passenger car is not deductible. The other 50% is deductible **without a road log** if the car serves taxable activity at least partly. A higher share (up to 100%) needs detailed records of business use. Where several cars are rented, the method can be chosen per car, which requires a per-car split of the fee on the contract — [NAV ÁFA guidance](https://nav.gov.hu/ado/afa/Tajekoztato_a_gazdasa20181228); [Deloitte HU](https://www.deloitte.com/hu/hu/services/tax/perspectives/meghosszabbitasra-kerult-a-cegautok-berletere-vonatkozo-50-szazalekos-velemezett-afalevonasi-hanyad-alkalmazhatosaga.html)
- The EU derogation behind the 50% rule (originally Council Implementing Decision 2018/1493, from 1 Jan 2019) was extended by **Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/3205 until 31 Dec 2027** — [adozona "EU-s határozat a személygépkocsi-bérletre"](https://adozona.hu/afa/EUs_hatarozat_a_szemelygepkocsiberletre_von_0ORHP8); [Deloitte HU](https://www.deloitte.com/hu/hu/services/tax/perspectives/meghosszabbitasra-kerult-a-cegautok-berletere-vonatkozo-50-szazalekos-velemezett-afalevonasi-hanyad-alkalmazhatosaga.html)
- Correction to a search summary: the Privátbankár article on the "50% without a log" proposal is from **11 Nov 2018** (the 2019 law change), **not July 2026**, even though a search summary dated it 2026 — [Privátbankár](https://privatbankar.hu/cikkek/vallalat/ceges-auto-olcsobb-es-egyszerubb-lehet-a-berles-322735.html)
- **2026 registration tax** (relevant only for buying a new import, shown for context): 141–180 kW: 188,000 Ft; 121–140 kW: 141,000 Ft — [Ayvens HU](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/rolunk/hirek/flottakezeles/2026-adoemelesek-es-szabalyozasi-valtozasok/)

### Inferences
- **A6 40 TDI (150 kW, Euro 6).** Euro 6 diesels normally carry environmental class 14 or 15, which gives **51,000 Ft/month = 612,000 Ft/year** cégautóadó. Confirm the class code on the registration certificate (field V.9). An older Euro 4/5 car in class 6–10 would pay 76,000 Ft/month. In a rental the owner pays this, and it is folded into the monthly fee.
- **Private person renting privately:** no cégautóadó (the owner bears it inside the fee), no VAT recovery, no cost deduction. The full gross fee, including 27% VAT, is the real cost.
- **Company (Kft.) renting:**
  - The net fee is a deductible cost.
  - 50% of the VAT is recoverable without a log. In practice that is about 13.5% of the net fee back, so the effective cost is about 113.5% of net instead of 127%.
  - The company does not pay cégautóadó itself because the rental company is the owner.
  - Under the long-standing Szja rule, private use of a car that is subject to cégautóadó does not create taxable természetbeni juttatás (benefit in kind) for the employee or owner. In a rental the cégautóadó is paid by the lessor, not the user. Practitioners generally treat this as satisfying the condition, but an adozona reader question on exactly this point had no clear answer in the search snippet, so **this should be confirmed with a tax adviser**.
- **Egyéni vállalkozó:**
  - Under actual-cost taxation (vállalkozói jövedelem szerinti), the rental fee can be claimed in proportion to business use, backed by a road log. The flat 500 km/month method is the alternative, but it covers only the km-based allowance, not the rental fee.
  - Because the car is owned by the rental company, the EV does not become a cégautóadó taxpayer.
  - VAT: the same 50% rule applies if the EV is in the VAT system (not alanyi adómentes, i.e. not VAT-exempt as a small business).
  - Átalányadózó (flat-rate) EVs cannot deduct actual costs, so renting gives them no tax advantage.
- **EV variant (e.g. A6 e-tron):** no cégautóadó, plus green-plate benefits. That saves about 612,000 Ft/year compared with the diesel if the car were owned by a company. In a rental the saving should show up as a lower fee only if the lessor passes it on.

- I could not open the official NAV page directly; the info sheet was read as a PDF mirrored on roadrecord.hu. The text matches the NAV notice reproduced by adozona and Ayvens.
- No primary source was fetched for the Szja tv. 70. § wording on private use of a rented car where the lessor pays cégautóadó.
- 2026 gépjárműadó Ft/kW rates were not retrieved (the adozona 2026 article returned HTTP 403).
- The environmental-class mapping (Euro 6 to class 14/15) is from general knowledge, not a fetched source.

## Q4. Cross-border: renting in Vienna/Bratislava and driving mainly in Hungary

### Takeaway
**Not a viable 12-month solution for a Hungarian resident.** Hungarian law bars Hungarian residents from driving foreign-plated cars in Hungary except in narrow cases:
- habitual residence abroad
- regular work abroad with the car
- short-term use (less than 60 days in any 6 months per a 2024 summary; 30 days in the original 2011 rule) with the keeper's written consent

The penalty is plate and registration confiscation on the spot plus a fine reported at up to 800,000 Ft. Rental contracts also restrict foreign use, and breaching them can void the insurance cover.

### Cited Findings
- General rule: "külföldi rendszámmal nem közlekedhet itthon magyar állampolgár" (a Hungarian citizen may not drive a foreign-plated car at home), except in narrow cases. The exceptions are:
  1. habitual residence outside Hungary, proven by a foreign authority document
  2. use in Hungary for **less than 60 days within any six-month period**, with the keeper's written consent stating the handover date
  3. use of the car abroad for regular work

  If the exemption cannot be proven on the spot, "a rendőr bevonja az autó külföldi rendszámát és forgalmiját" (the police withdraw the foreign plate and registration) and imposes an administrative fine. "A mentességet utólag nem lehet igazolni" (the exemption cannot be proven afterwards). A central register tracks claimed exemptions, so the 60-day count can be checked — [Economx, Aug 2024](https://www.economx.hu/gazdasag/2024/08/14/kulfoldi-rendszam-hasznalata-itthon-szabalyok-794542/)
- Earlier sources (the 2011 rule) give the short-use exemption as **under 30 days** with the owner's written consent, proven by a public or fully probative private document. The burden of proof is on the driver — [adozona 2011](https://adozona.hu/kommentar/20110909_auto_szabaly_szeptember_auto_kulfoldi_ren); [adozona 2011-11](https://adozona.hu/cegauto_es_regisztracios_ado/20111101_kulfoldi_rendszam_auto_gepjarmu_torveny). This **conflicts** with the 60-day/6-month figure in the 2024 Economx article; the later rule likely reflects an amendment.
- Fines **up to 800,000 Ft** — [Privátbankár](https://privatbankar.hu/cikkek/ado/raszallnak-megint-a-kulfoldi-rendszamokra-800-ezer-is-lehet-a-birsag-245174.html). An estimated **40,000** Hungarians drive foreign-plated cars, mostly for tax optimisation — [Privátbankár](https://privatbankar.hu/cikkek/ado/kulfoldi-rendszamok-eddig-13-autost-fuleltek-le-a-hajsza-soran-246230.html). One search summary dated a NAV enforcement campaign article to 2026; I did not confirm that date.
- An ombudsman intervention and a CJEU ruling softened enforcement for residents who use a car lawfully provided by a **foreign employer** — [Autonavigator](https://www.autonavigator.hu/sztori/ombudsman-vet-veget-a-kulfoldi-rendszamok-uldozesenek). This is reported via secondary summary; it does not cover a self-arranged foreign rental.
- Foreign-plated cars on which costs are claimed in Hungary are also subject to cégautóadó, so the tax cannot be avoided this way — [NAV info sheet](https://www.roadrecord.hu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/31-cegautoado-es-az-ahhoz-kapcsolodo-szja-szabalyok-20260122.pdf)
- Austrian/German subscription providers (Sixt+) restrict foreign use by country zone and require advance notice plus a **€9.99/month** fee. A breach can trigger a contractual penalty and loss of protection cover — [Sixt+ DE help center](https://www.sixt.de/plus/help-center/articles/fahrzeugnutzung/)
- Price level: German-market A6 subscriptions run about **€899–1,649/month** (see Q3). No Austria-specific A6 subscription prices were found — [AUTO BILD](https://www.autobild.de/artikel/audi-a6-hybrid-auto-abo-laufzeit-preis-mieten-22246729.html); [Mivodo](https://www.mivodo.com/auto-abo/marken/audi/a6/a6-allroad/)

### Inferences

## Q5. Market context 2026: HUF/EUR rate and price trends

### Takeaway
The forint has strengthened during 2026: from about 385 HUF/EUR at end-2025 and about 391 in March, to about 350 in mid-June and about **364 in mid-October 2026**.

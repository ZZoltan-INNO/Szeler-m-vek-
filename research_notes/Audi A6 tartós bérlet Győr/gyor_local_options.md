# Local options in and around Győr for 12-month rental of an Audi A6-class car (tartós bérlet / hosszú távú bérlés)

Research date: 2026-10-29. About 25 tool calls in total. Several key sites could not be read: tartosberlet.carmenrent.hu and premiumberautogyor.hu returned HTTP 503, and the Porsche Hungaria finance page returned 404. Nothing below has been confirmed by phone. Treat every price as indicative, and confirm availability and the current fee with each company.

## Q1: Which independent rental companies operate in Győr, do they have A6, 5-series, E-class, Superb or Passat class cars, and what do they charge per month?

### Takeaway
The best local match is **CarMen Rent Kft. (Győr, Nádor tér 1.)**. Its main business is long-term rental (tartós bérlet) and fleet management, mostly with new cars. It has also verifiably rented Audi A6s (and an A8) to the Győr municipality at about **416,665 HUF + VAT per car per month (2024)** and **446,000 HUF per month (2023 report)**. The other Győr firms either publish prices only for compact cars (EURO-RENT) or list premium cars without prices (HSCS Rent, Prémium Autóbérlés Győr). No company publishes a 12-month price for an A6, 5-series or E-class.

## Q2: Do the Győr brand dealers (Audi, VW, Skoda, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Volvo) offer rental, long-term rental or operative lease?

### Takeaway
**Porsche Győr** (the Audi/VW/Skoda dealer, run by Porsche Inter Auto Hungaria Kft.) lists "Bérautó" (rental car) among its services. Long-term rental and operative lease for the VW Group brands go through **Porsche Lízing és Szolgáltató Kft.** (Porsche Pénzügyi csoport), which advertises tartós bérlet aimed at businesses. No dealer publishes a 12-month A6, 5-series or E-class offer. The **Mercedes-Benz / Volvo / Kia dealer Autentik Motor-Car** advertises fleet management but no rental. I found no BMW Győr rental or long-term rental offer.

## Q3: Are there Audi Hungaria-related (employee, ex-plant, young used car) rental or lease options in Győr?

### Takeaway
I found **no verifiable public source** for an Audi Hungaria employee, ex-plant or ex-fleet car rental or lease programme open to the public. The one documented "Audi in Győr" rental story concerns the municipality's A6s, and those were rented from CarMen Rent, **not** from Audi Hungaria.

### Cited Findings
- 444.hu stated explicitly that the city's cars were not provided by Audi Hungaria but rented from CarMen Rent Kft. — [444.hu, 16 Aug 2023](https://444.hu/2023/08/16/majdnem-egymillio-forintot-fizet-havonta-a-gyori-onkormanyzat-dezsi-csaba-andras-luxusautojaert)
- Background: Audi Hungaria had 11,663 employees at the end of 2023. A July 2026 Telex article reported that the company expects flexibility (redeployment) from engine-plant staff, with no plant closure or layoffs officially announced. Neither source mentions an employee car programme. — [Telex, 14 Jul 2026](https://telex.hu/gazdasag/2026/07/14/audi-hungaria-jarmugyartas-motorgyartas-audi-q3-cupra-terramar-mebeco)

### Inferences
- Young used Audis in Győr are realistically sourced through Porsche Győr's Das WeltAuto / hasznaltauto.hu stock or CarMen Autó's used-car stock. These are sales channels, though, not rental.

### Gaps
- Employee or internal programmes, if they exist, are not publicly documented. I did not find any company that rents out ex-Audi-Hungaria plant or fleet cars.

## Q4: Can a used premium car (e.g. a 2–5-year-old A6) be rented from a local firm for 12 months, and at what price? Is delivery possible?

### Takeaway
No Győr firm publishes a used-premium 12-month rental price. Delivery is published only by EURO-RENT (3,500 HUF + VAT within Győr; 300 HUF/km + VAT elsewhere), and its fleet has no A6-class car. HSCS Rent requires pickup at its branch.

### Cited Findings
- EURO-RENT delivery: within Győr 3,500 HUF + VAT; elsewhere 300 HUF/km + VAT; out-of-hours handover 3,500 HUF + VAT. — [EURO-RENT árak](https://www.rentcargyor.hu/berauto_kategoriak_arak.html)
- HSCS Rent: pickup and return at Hédervári út 81. No delivery service is mentioned. A deposit is required. — [HSCS Rent](https://hscsrent.hu/)
- CarMen Rent mostly uses new vehicles for its long-term rentals, and it separately sells used cars. — [CarMen Rent – Rólunk](https://www.carmenrent.hu/rolunk.html)

### Inferences
- The local benchmark for an A6 on long-term rental is about 417–446k HUF net per month (municipal contracts, 2023–2024; new or near-new cars implied but not stated). A used A6 might be cheaper, but no source confirms this.

### Gaps
- No public price for a 12-month rental of a 2–5-year-old A6 from any local firm. It must be requested from CarMen Rent, HSCS Rent, Nettos and Prémium Autóbérlés Győr.
- Delivery terms for CarMen Rent, Nettos and Prémium Autóbérlés are unknown.
- Mosonmagyaróvár, Sopron and Tatabánya/Komárom remain uncovered; I found no premium long-term rental firm there.

# National / international providers for a ~12-month rental of an Audi A6-class car (Hungary, focus Győr)

Research date: 2026-10-29. All HUF prices are copied from the source. "net" = + ÁFA (27% VAT); the gross figures marked "(calc.)" are my own ×1.27 arithmetic, not published numbers. Sources were fetched on 2026-10-29 unless they are flagged as older.

## 1. Which providers really offer 12-month rental of premium cars in Hungary (2025-2026)?

### Takeaway
Five providers have a current, verifiable 1–12+ month product in Hungary with premium (E-segment) cars in the fleet or price list: **Hertz Hungary (tartósbérlet, 1–12 hónap)**, **Ayvens Flex (1–24 hónap, companies)**, **SIXT+ autó-előfizetés (1/3/6/12 hónap, private and company)**, **Europcar Hungary (havi számlázású bérlet)** and **Budget Hungary "Mini Lease" (1–12 hónap, private and company)**. I found no Hungary-specific product page for Avis Flex, Arval Mid Term Rental or Arval Flex. Classic operating lease (operatív lízing / tartós bérlet) firms such as NextAuto, NetLízing, Sajó Car Fleet and EasyLease quote 36–60 month terms, mostly to companies only.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- For an **A6-equivalent on exactly 12 months**, the only published, current HUF prices are **Hertz E-Class ~671 k Ft + ÁFA/month** and **Ayvens Flex "Exclusive" (BMW 5) 566 k Ft/month** (most likely net). For a cheaper upper-D fallback there are Hertz Passat Variant at 447 k + ÁFA, Ayvens "Deluxe" Passat/Superb at 335 k, and SIXT+ BMW 420d Gran Coupé at 428 k net per 28 days.
- Europcar and Budget have the right product type but publish no prices, so they need a quote. Europcar's included delivery and 3 000 km/month make it attractive for a Győr-based company.

### Gaps
- I found no current Hungarian SIXT+, Budget or Europcar price for an A6, 5 Series or E-Class. Hertz's "vagy hasonló" (or similar) means the exact model is not guaranteed.
- Arval, Athlon, Mobilease, Fix Rent, Lenti and Wigo: I could not verify any Hungarian 12-month premium product for them (not searched in depth for the last four). GreenGo is a per-minute car-sharing service in Budapest and is not relevant for Győr (from background knowledge, not verified this session).
- Avis Hungary: the monthly or long-term product is unconfirmed.

## 3. What is included: insurance, maintenance, km, deposit, excess, cross-border use

### Takeaway
Mobility-style products (Hertz, Ayvens Flex, SIXT+, Europcar, Budget) include KGFB, Casco or damage/theft cover, maintenance, seasonal tyres, assistance and usually the Hungarian motorway vignette. Km allowances are 2 000 km/month (Ayvens), 3 000 km/month (Europcar) or chosen by the customer (SIXT+). Deposits and cross-border rules are mostly not published.

### Cited Findings
- **SIXT+**: the fee includes KGFB and Casco, maintenance and repair, seasonal preparation, 0–24 assistance **in Hungary**, a replacement car, and the chosen monthly km allowance. Excess (önrész) is shown per car and "A limit csökkenthető" (can be reduced). Glass and tyre protection costs 76 200 Ft gross. Overmileage is charged at the end of the contract at the category rate (rate not published). The monthly km can be lowered at each monthly turn, or raised for a surcharge. Other gross fees: admin fee for fines or damage 13 000 Ft; cleaning 13 000 / 25 000 Ft; odour neutralisation 63 500 Ft — [sixtplus.hu](https://sixtplus.hu/), [sixtplus.hu/arlista](https://sixtplus.hu/arlista), [BMW 420d page](https://sixtplus.hu/bmw-420d-xdrive-grancoupe-csz366)
- **Ayvens Flex**: maintenance, insurance, taxes, admin, 24h assistance, a replacement car (repair longer than one day) and motorway vignettes; 2000 km allowance per category (4000 for vans) — [Ayvens Flex HU](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/flottakezeles/szolgaltatasaink/short-term-rental/); [price data](https://autotartosberlet.ayvens.com/rovidtavu-autoberles)
- **Europcar**: 3 000 km/hó, full insurance against damage and theft with optional excess buy-down, repair costs, teljesítményadó, Hungarian motorways, replacement car, 24h assistance, and delivery and collection — [europcar.com/hu-hu](https://www.europcar.com/hu-hu/p/car-rental/by-duration)
- **Budget Mini Lease**: yearly Hungarian e-toll, all compulsory taxes and KGFB, maintenance and damage repair, winter tyres, and 24/7 assistance in Hungary (2025) — [XpatLoop](https://xpatloop.com/channels/2025/specials/monthly-car-rental-special-offer-from-budget-rent-a-car-hungary.html)
- **Hertz**: assistance across Europe, return at any Hertz office in Europe, full insurance with optional excess buy-down, annual vignette, free km in most categories. The tartósbérlet FAQ (what the fee includes, car swap, km overrun) is on the page, but the answers did not load in my fetch — [hertzhungary.hu](https://hertzhungary.hu/)
- **Kaució example**: PerfectRent asks a 400 000 Ft deposit for its monthly A6 — [perfectrent.hu](https://perfectrent.hu/berelheto-jarmuveink/nagy-auto/audi-a6/)
- **Operating lease (NextAuto)**: Casco excess of 10%, minimum 100 000 Ft; cégautóadó, gépjárműadó and baleseti adó included — [nextauto.hu](https://www.nextauto.hu/ajanlatok/tartos-berlet/1596193199/)
- Private-person km rule seen in one search snippet: "magánszemély bérlő esetén érvényesek 2500 km / hónap esetén" (from an unidentified provider's Volvo listing; not verified)

### Inferences
- Hertz's "Európa-szerte" assistance and Europe-wide return suggest that cross-border use (AT/SK) is foreseen. SIXT+ and Budget assistance is explicitly "magyarországi" (Hungary only), so trips to Austria or Slovakia must be cleared in the contract.
- Cégautóadó matters only for company-registered use. In rental products it is either included (Europcar's teljesítményadó, Ayvens' "taxes") or not mentioned.

### Gaps
- Deposits (kaució) for Hertz, Ayvens, SIXT+, Europcar and Budget are not published.
- Overmileage fees per km are not published by any of the five main providers.
- Cross-border permission and fees are not published for any of them.

## 5. Private individual vs company eligibility; contract flexibility

### Takeaway
A **private person can rent for 12 months** from SIXT+ (explicitly the same offers as companies), Budget Mini Lease (explicitly private persons and entrepreneurs) and very likely Hertz (company name optional on the tartósbérlet form, "Magán vagy üzleti célú" rental). Ayvens Flex and Europcar's monthly product are marketed to businesses. Operating lease houses such as NextAuto exclude private persons.

### Cited Findings
- **SIXT+**: "Magánszemélyek is használhatják a Sixt+-t és ugyanazokat az ajánlatokat kapják, mint a cégek? Igen" (private persons get the same offers as companies). The customer is bound for the chosen period ("szerződéses kötelezettséget vállal"). km and terms can be adjusted monthly. A longer commitment gives a lower fixed fee — [sixtplus.hu](https://sixtplus.hu/)
- **Budget Mini Lease**: "available for private individuals and entrepreneurs"; 1–12 months, extendable (2025) — [XpatLoop](https://xpatloop.com/channels/2025/specials/monthly-car-rental-special-offer-from-budget-rent-a-car-hungary.html)
- **Hertz**: "Magán vagy üzleti célú, hazai vagy nemzetközi autóbérlés" (private or business rental). The tartósbérlet request form marks name, phone and email as required, with "Cégnév" (company name) optional — [hertzhungary.hu](https://hertzhungary.hu/), [hertzhungary.hu/berles](https://hertzhungary.hu/berles)
- **Ayvens Flex**: business-oriented; can be extended or returned at any time without penalty — [Ayvens Flex HU](https://www.ayvens.com/hu-hu/flottakezeles/szolgaltatasaink/short-term-rental/)
- **Europcar**: no commitment, renews month to month; the rental fee is deductible as a company cost (company-oriented wording) — [europcar.com/hu-hu](https://www.europcar.com/hu-hu/p/car-rental/by-duration)
- **NextAuto**: "Magánszemélyeknek nem tudunk bérleti konstrukciót biztosítani" (no leases for private persons) — [nextauto.hu](https://www.nextauto.hu/ajanlatok/tartos-berlet/1596193199/)

### Inferences
- A private person in Győr has three realistic routes: Hertz Győr (local; E-Class class or Passat fallback), SIXT+ (BMW 420d Gran Coupé or similar; pickup in Budapest or paid delivery) and Budget Mini Lease (quote needed).
- A KKV or egyéni vállalkozó (sole trader) can also use Ayvens Flex (BMW 5 class at 566 k Ft/month) and Europcar, both with penalty-free return or month-to-month renewal. Net pricing plus VAT recovery makes these the cheapest E-segment option for a company.

### Gaps

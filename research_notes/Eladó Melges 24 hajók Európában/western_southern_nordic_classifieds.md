# Melges 24 for sale on national classifieds and local sailing sites: Western, Southern and Northern Europe (checked 2026-11-21)

Scope: Italy, France, Spain, Portugal, Monaco, Greece, Malta, UK, Ireland, Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland. Big pan-European portals (YachtWorld, boat24, TheYachtMarket, Apollo Duck) and IM24CA/Melges Europe classifieds belong to other researchers. Listings from those portals that turned up here are marked "OVERLAP".

Method note: Several of the most important national classifieds block automated access. subito.it returned 403 (Akamai "Access Denied"), as did leboncoin.fr, donedeal.ie, botentekoop.nl, car.gr, bakeca.it, annoncesbateau.com, inautia.com, rightboat.com and dailyboats.com. milanuncios.com returned 405. Apollo Duck and Boatshed served bot-wall pages. Wallapop renders only with JavaScript. For these sites the details below come from search-engine snippets, and in several cases only the search-results page URL could be recorded, not the individual ad URL. Each such record is flagged. I did not try to get around the blocks.

## Q1. Italy: subito.it, the Italian Melges 24 class site and other Italian portals

### Takeaway
Italy has by far the most Melges 24 ads in this region. Snippets from Subito.it show roughly 15 boat ads, priced at about €12,500–45,000 and clustered around Trieste and Monfalcone, the Veneto and Lake Maggiore. Subito blocks automated access, so no individual ad URL or posting date could be verified. The only Italian ad verified first-hand, with an exact URL and a date, is ITA 265 on the Italian class site (posted 2026-08-28, no price shown).

### Inferences
- The Subito snippets likely double-count some boats. For example, ITA 598 "on Lake Maggiore" is probably one of the Laveno-Mombello or Sesto Calende ads, since both towns are on Lake Maggiore. - The ads described as available after the Trieste Worlds suggest a wave of listings after the 2025 Worlds. Some of these may have sold by November 2026.
- ITA 265 is an early hull. Melges 24 production began in 1993 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melges_24)), so a hull in the 200s is probably a 1994–95 boat, but the ad does not confirm this.

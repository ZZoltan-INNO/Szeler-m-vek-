Scope: Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Hungary, Slovenia, Croatia, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Poland, Romania, Bulgaria, Serbia and the Baltics; national classifieds plus local sailing and class sites. Method: direct searches on the classifieds where reachable (curl/WebFetch on 2026-11-21), multilingual web searches, and checks of every candidate detail page.

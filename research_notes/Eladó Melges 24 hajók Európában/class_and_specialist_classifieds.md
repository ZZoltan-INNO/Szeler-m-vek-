# Melges 24 boats for sale in Europe: class-association and one-design-specialist classifieds (checked 2026-11-21)

Scope note: these notes cover the class association sites (IM24CA, the national classes), the builder and dealer sites (Melges, Melges Europe, Melges UK, the Dutch dealer) and the specialist or racing-boat classifieds and forums. The big marketplaces (boat24/boot24, YachtWorld, Apollo Duck, Rightboat and others) and the national general classifieds (FINN, Subito, ricardo and others) belong to other researchers. Where those sites came up, they are listed below as "overlaps", marked unverified.

**Bottom line:** only **2 confirmed, currently active European Melges 24 listings** exist on class or specialist sources: **ITA 265** (Italian class site, posted 2026-08-28) and **ITA 693** (US class classifieds, boat in Italy, posted 2026-04-21). The European class and dealer channels hold very little stock. The real market sits on Facebook (the official IM24CA "Marketplace" is a Facebook group, which can't be read without logging in) and on the big marketplaces. The 8–12 target can't be met from class or specialist sources alone.

## Key question 1: Which Melges 24s are listed on IM24CA (melges24.com) and on the European national class association sites?

### Takeaway
IM24CA has no classifieds of its own. Its "Marketplace" menu points to a Facebook group and to an external Membos marketplace, which returned HTTP 502 on every attempt. Of the European national class sites, only the Italian class (melges24.it) has an active boat-for-sale ad (ITA 265, August 2026).

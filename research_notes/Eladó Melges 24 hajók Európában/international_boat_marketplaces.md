# Melges 24 for sale in Europe on international/pan-European boat marketplaces (research date 2026-11-21)

Scope note: this covers boat24 (all language versions), YachtWorld/boats.com, Boatshop24, TheYachtMarket, Apollo Duck (.com/.co.uk/.ie), Yachtall, Band of Boats, Scanboat, Rightboat, iNautia, TopBoats, Sailboatlistings, Youboat, plus the aggregators Listingsport and yacht.de, which mirrors boat24. Many portals block automated access, with HTTP 403, Cloudflare or Akamai pages, or an Anubis proof-of-work wall. For those I used search-engine snippets, and I flag that the details are unverified. **Only about 5–7 distinct European Melges 24s look plausibly live on these portals. That is below the 10–15 target because current inventory on the big portals is thin, not because of missed searching.** Most boats that show up in searches have already been removed, sold or deactivated.

## Q1. Which Melges 24 listings located in Europe are on the major international boat marketplaces?

### Takeaway
On 2026-11-21 the large pan-European portals carry very few live Melges 24s. boat24 has exactly one used Melges 24 (Lago Maggiore, IT). Scanboat has one (Schleswig, DE). iNautia/TopBoats appear to have one to three, in Monfalcone IT, La Trinité-sur-Mer FR and possibly NL/ES, but these could not be opened. ### Inferences
- The Melges 24 second-hand market in Europe mostly runs through class channels (IM24CA, Melges Europe) and national classifieds rather than the big brokerage portals. Boats that do reach boat24 or iNautia tend to sell or be withdrawn within months: 3 of the 4 boat24 Melges 24 IDs found are already gone.
- Boats Group sites (YachtWorld, boats.com, iNautia, TopBoats) share listing IDs. The iNautia ID 9888905 has the same format as YachtWorld IDs, yet the boat does not appear in YachtWorld's Melges search. It is probably filed under the make "White Formula" and/or shown only on the regional (FR/ES) front-ends.
- European boats are often listed under the builder rather than "Melges": White Formula (UK, 1990s), Devoti (2000s), or "Melges 24 OD". Searching only make=Melges misses them.

### Gaps
- Could not open TheYachtMarket, Yachtall, Boatshop24, iNautia, TopBoats, Rightboat, Band of Boats detail pages or apolloduck.co.uk (403, JavaScript rendering or anti-bot walls). Their contents rest on search snippets and may be stale.
- Did not check boot24/Bootsbörse, Yachtsnet or "Sailing Solutions" because I ran out of tool budget. Bootsbörse-type portals and the Kleinanzeigen family are covered by other researchers.
- Band of Boats listing URLs (numeric `/en/boats-for-sale/NNNNNN`) for the La Trinité 1995 and Morgan Marine 1998 boats could not be retrieved.
